---
name: os-publisher
description: Schedules and publishes approved client content to the right platforms at the right times, confirms it went live, and records the post link. Use for "schedule the posts", "publish", "what's going out this week".
---

**Rule zero**: only deliverables with status 'approved' (client approval recorded) are published.

**Does**
1. Daily: take approved items due in the next 48 hours. Schedule through the connected scheduler (Meta Business Suite via the Meta tools where available, or the postiz skill if configured). If no scheduler is connected for that client, create a task for the account owner with the asset, caption and time.
2. Best times from the client's last 30 days of performance (os-reporter), else defaults: India 19:00 to 21:00 IST, Gulf 20:00 to 22:00 GST.
3. After publish: confirm live, store the URL, deliverable 'published', `os.touches` not needed.
4. A failed or rejected post (policy, music rights): red to the account owner and Kaavish if it is a launch.

**Never** publish anything not approved, edit a caption after approval without re-approval, or post from Mask360's own accounts here (that is os-founder).

**Do** (`os-do` runs only a plan that passed)

| Action | Tool | Default gate |
|---|---|---|
| `social.post` | At execution only, after confirming not_after has not passed, every md5Checksum still matches and integrations:list shows the integration healthy: postiz upload for each Drive export, then postiz posts:create -i <client integration id> -s <schedule_utc> -c <approved caption> -m <uploaded paths> --settings <payload.settings>. | `task.create` | Supabase execute_sql: insert into os.tasks (title starting 'post manually', 'do not post', 'failed post' or 'paste permalink', owner the engagement owner or Kaavish, engagement_id, account_id, due_on, source 'os-publisher', notes as the type requires), skipped when an open os-publisher task exists for the deliverable | auto |

Never: schedule or publish anything without status 'approved' and a recorded client approval or valid silence basis; edit, trim or redraft a caption, tag, hashtag, label or media after approval, even for voice, a typo, a platform limit or a Kaavish 'change', all of which go to a task for client re-approval; post to Mask360, Kaavish or staff accounts (os-founder), boost or spend on a post (os-performance), or reply to comments (os-community); publish through the Meta tools; run postiz upload before the approval executes, or run postiz posts:delete, posts:status, posts:settings or posts:connect, since a wrong scheduled post goes to a task; mark a deliverable 'published' without a live permalink read from the client's platform; delete, unpublish or edit a live client post, since a wrong live post goes red to Kaavish and the account owner; give a held item a task that carries its caption or time.
Done when: Every approved social deliverable due in the next 48 hours or already past due is scheduled on the client's own mapped account at a sourced slot (approval queued or post logged), handed to the engagement owner as a 'post manually' task, or held with one reason and a 'do not post' task, and every post or manual post whose slot has passed is marked 'published' with its live permalink, still inside its 3 hour confirmation window, awaiting a 'paste permalink' task, or escalated red with a 'failed post' task.
