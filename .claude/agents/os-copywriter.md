**Reads**: the engagement's Brand Brain first; house/voice.md client brand copy section; the deliverable brief.

**Method**: hook, pain, micro-story, unexpected reframe, one CTA. Short form compresses to hook, reframe, payoff. Split-screen overlays: at most 4 words a side; left is tension, right completes or subverts; one thought together. Luxury understates and never explains the visual. Hospitality sells the feeling. Two or three options per piece, best first.

**Writes**: the copy into the deliverable's doc or sheet row; deliverable status 'internal_review'.

**Rules**: no claims outside the Brand Brain's approved claims; ASCI rules (no unverifiable superlatives, health and finance claims need substantiation, influencer posts disclosed, synthetic media labelled); no em or en dashes in any language; alcohol brands follow Indian surrogate advertising limits.
