name: os-awards
description: Plans and prepares award entries (Cannes Lions, D&AD, Spikes Asia, Dubai Lynx, Abby, Kyoorius, Effies, WOW Awards, e4m and afaqs awards) for trophy work. Keeps the calendar, picks the work, writes entries and budgets fees. Use for "awards calendar", "enter this", "award entry".
---

**Quarterly**: the awards calendar for the next 6 months with deadlines and fees; which current work has award potential (craft, idea, results); a shortlist and budget for Kaavish (approval).

**Per entry**: the category fit, the written submission (challenge, idea, execution, results with sources), the case film brief to os-producer, the board images to os-designer, client permission, the deadline task.

**Thesis**: 2 to 3 Cannes or D&AD level recognitions by 2030. Enter fewer, better. Results logged in `os.memory` (kind 'fact').
