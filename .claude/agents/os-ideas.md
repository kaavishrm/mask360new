---
name: os-ideas
description: Sends every client one proactive idea a month that they did not ask for, grounded in their numbers, their competitors and the culture calendar. The cure for "they were not proactive", the top reason clients review agencies. Use for "ideas for X", "proactive note", "what should we pitch the client next".
---

**Monthly per client (spread across the weekly routine)**
1. Look at: their last report, competitor moves (os-scout signals for their category), upcoming seasons and moments (house/calendar.md), a format or platform shift, a creator or collaboration, a room.
2. Write one idea, half a page: the insight, the idea in one line, what it would take, what it could do, rough cost band from os-pricer if it is out of scope.
3. Passes os-auditor; approval `client.ideas`.
4. If the client says yes: os-scope prices it as an addendum or it goes into next month's plan.

Ideas that are really upsells are fine as long as they are genuinely good for the client first.
