---
name: os-ideas
description: Sends every client one proactive idea a month that they did not ask for, grounded in their numbers, their competitors and the culture calendar. The cure for "they were not proactive", the top reason clients review agencies. Use for "ideas for X", "proactive note", "what should we pitch the client next".
---

**Monthly per client (spread across the weekly routine)**
1. Look at: their last report, competitor moves (os-scout signals for their category), upcoming seasons and moments (house/calendar.md), a format or platform shift, a creator or collaboration, a room.
2. Write one idea, half a page: the insight, the idea in one line, what it would take, what it could do, rough cost band from os-pricer if it is out of scope.
3. Passes os-auditor; approval `client.ideas`.
4. If the client says yes: os-scope prices it as an addendum or it goes into next month's plan.

Ideas that are really upsells are fine as long as they are genuinely good for the client first.

Never: quote a firm price, offer a discount, or draft an addendum or proposal, since bands come only from an approved os-pricer change_order and extra scope goes to os-scope; start work on an idea, create deliverables, put it in a content plan or assign the team, since that follows the client's written yes through os-scope; put one client's numbers, results, plans, creators or files, or credentials beyond the profile.md proof wording, into another client's note, thread or Drive folder; send a second idea to the same account in one calendar month, or write to anyone outside the engagement's client_contacts (sibling brands in the group go through os-growth); show vendor costs, margin or internal rates; share a Drive file with the client or put a Drive link or attachment in the note; create a second Gmail draft for one note.
Done when: every engagement read is either in this run's plan with exactly one verified client.ideas approval (the full note in its body, one Gmail draft, the internal doc in a Mask360-only folder) or in plan.skips with a reason; by the month's last weekly run every eligible client account has had one note queued or sent this month in Asia/Kolkata or a logged skip; every unconditional client yes that os-inbox did not route has one os-scope task; and every os-ideas task the plan answered is closed.
