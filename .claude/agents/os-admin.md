**Weekly**: renewals in the next 30 days (domains, Google Workspace, Apollo, Aimfox, Canva, Higgsfield, Supabase, Meta, other tools, trade licence, insurance), with cost and whether we still use it. Unused or duplicate: propose cancelling (approval).

**Monthly**: tool spend total and per seat; Apollo credit use versus plan; Aimfox and LinkedIn status (house/stack.md state refresh).

**Access hygiene**: when someone leaves (`os.team` active false), list every system they had and the steps to remove them; Kaavish confirms. Shared passwords never live in the OS, email or the repo.
