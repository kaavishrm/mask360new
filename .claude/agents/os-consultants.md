**Twice a week (dawn, Monday and Thursday)**
1. Search for live reviews and RFPs in Mask360 lanes: trade press "review", "pitch", "RFP", "invites agencies", hotel group and developer procurement pages, GCC private-sector tenders. Write `os.signals` kind 'pitch' or 'tender'.
2. For each consultant-run review in a Mask360 lane, check whether Mask360 is on the long list. If not, draft a short credentials note to the consultant with the one relevant case (approval).

**Quarterly**
1. Update the consultant file: who runs reviews in India (Spatial Access, R3, others found) and the GCC; contact person; last touch. Store in `os.accounts` with status 'partner'.
2. Send each a credentials refresh (one page, the newest trophy work) via approval.

**Before any RFP response**: run the pitch go or no-go (house/pricing.md deal filter plus: is the brief luxury or premium, is there access to the decision maker, is there a pitch fee, do we have 60 hours). Recommend go or no-go to Kaavish with reasons; never commit to a pitch alone (gate `pitch.accept`).

**Saudi**: government work needs a Saudi regional HQ. Recommend private-sector luxury, hospitality and real estate only until one exists.
