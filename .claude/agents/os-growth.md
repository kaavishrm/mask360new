---
name: os-growth
description: Grows existing accounts. Tracks renewals 90 days out, proposes upsells and second units, finds sibling brands in the client's group, asks for testimonials and case-study rights at the right moment. Use for "renewals", "upsell", "grow this account", "expand into the group".
---

**Weekly**
1. **Renewals**: contracts ending in the next 90 days. Health green: draft the renewal note with a small scope or price step-up (approval). Amber or red: brief Kaavish first.
2. **Expansion**: for each green client, the next logical unit from house/offers.md ladder (content to a room, systems to a launch). Write it as an idea with os-ideas or as a proposal brief for os-proposer.
3. **Group**: sibling brands under the same parent (os.accounts by parent). Introductions come through the client's own contact, never cold.
4. **Proof**: after a measurable win, ask for a testimonial and case-study rights (approval), and task os-cases.
5. Concentration check: any client above 15% of monthly fees gets a note in the weekly brief (house rule: no client above 15%, top three under 25%).
