---
name: os-qbr
description: Runs quarterly outcome reviews with every retained client: results against KPIs, what we learned, the next quarter's plan, and the conversation about expanding the relationship. Use for "QBR", "quarterly review", "outcome review".
---

**In January, April, July and October (monthly routine)**
1. Schedule a 45-minute review with each retained client's senior contact (approval `calendar.book`).
2. Build the deck (Google Slides or Doc in the client's Reports folder, templates/qbr.md): the quarter's three outcomes against KPIs, what worked, what we changed, the plan for next quarter, one bigger idea (a second unit, a room, a launch), and the renewal date if within 6 months.
3. Prep note for Kaavish or the account owner: the client's mood (os-health), open issues, what to ask for.
4. After: decisions and asks into `os.memory` and tasks; upsell interest to os-growth.

**Verify** (`os-verify` tries to refute the plan before anything happens)

**Do** (`os-do` runs only a plan that passed)

| Action | Tool | Default gate |
|---|---|---|
| `calendar.book` | mcp__Supabase__execute_sql insert into os.approvals in every mode, whatever the gate (action 'calendar.book', agent 'os-qbr', title '<Account> quarterly review, Q<n> <yyyy>', expires_at 72 hours ahead) with payload calendar_id (the literal os.orgs.settings.stack.calendar_id), event (summary, description, startTime, endTime, timeZone 'Asia/Kolkata', attendees, guestPermissions with guestsCanInviteOthers and guestsCanModify false, visibility 'private', no attachments), account_id and engagement_ids; os-gatekeeper calls mcp__Google_Calendar__create_event with that payload once Kaavish approves | notify |
| `doc.create` | mcp__Google_Drive__create_file as a Google Doc from textContent: the deck (all seven templates/qbr.md sections) in M360 OS/Clients/<Brand>/Reports when get_file_permissions on it shows only @mask360.agency, else in M360 OS/Briefs, and the prep note in M360 OS/Briefs; mcp__Google_Drive__update_file only to complete a deck's pending month in place; never mcp__Google_Drive__share_file | auto |
| `brief.owner` | mcp__Gmail__send_message to os.orgs.owner_email only, subject '[M360 OS] qbr prep: <Account>', with the prep note link, the notice deadline and one line per engagement skipped for an exit, dispute or legal signal | auto |

| `db.write` | mcp__Supabase__execute_sql insert into os.memory with kind 'decision' only, subject the account name and source the QBR event id plus the notes doc id, at most 5 rows per review, skipped when an active row with the same subject and source exists | auto |

Never: send, share or attach the QBR deck or the prep note to the client or anyone outside Mask360, since the deck is shown in the meeting and anything sent afterwards goes through os-status; call create_event directly or book a review without Kaavish's approval code, whatever the notify gate or the mode; put a price, fee, discount, notice deadline, renewal term beyond the date or new scope commitment in the deck or the invite, since upsell interest goes to os-growth and extra scope to os-scope as tasks; record a decision or ask that is not in the meeting notes, or write os.memory as any kind other than 'decision'; schedule a review for a project, diagnostic or event engagement, invite anyone outside client_contacts or at a sibling brand in the client's group, or invite Mask360 staff other than Kaavish and the account owner; change os.contracts, os.engagements, os.deals or another agent's records.
Done when: Every retained account due this quarter has exactly one QBR on the owner calendar after its approval executed, or one pending, unexpired calendar.book approval (an expired or failed one requeued fresh, a rejected one left alone); one sourced deck covering all its retained engagements in a Mask360-only folder, complete for all three months before the review; and a prep note in M360 OS/Briefs with Kaavish or the account owner. Every review held since the last run (the latest os-qbr do step with status done) has at most 5 decisions in os.memory, its asks as owned and dated tasks, out-of-scope asks with os-scope and upsell interest with os-growth, or a notes task for the account owner. Or the thinker returns an empty plan with a skip reason.
