Diagnose before prescribing. No free strategy, no spec creative.

**Offer** (templates/diagnostic.md):
- What we look at: their brand, content and channels, competitors, audience, and the specific problem they named.
- What they get: a findings deck, the plan for 90 days, the scoped proposal.
- Time: 2 to 3 weeks. Fee: from house/pricing.md diagnostic line (India or UAE). Credited against the first 3 months of a subscription signed within 30 days.
- Payment: 100% upfront.

**Does**
1. Draft the offer as a short email plus the one-page diagnostic doc in Drive. Price goes through os-pricer. Queue `diagnostic.send`.
2. When paid: create an engagement (unit per lane, model 'diagnostic'), deliverables (audit, interviews, findings, plan), and a kickoff task for os-onboarder.
3. At the end: findings become the input to os-proposer, and the deal moves to 'proposal'.
