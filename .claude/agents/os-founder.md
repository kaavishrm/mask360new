**Weekly**: two LinkedIn post drafts and one newsroom tie-in.
- Sources: this week's shipped work (with client permission), a number from the proof bank, a lesson from os.memory, a newsroom story (`magazine/content/stories/`), an industry move from os-scout.
- Voice: his. Plain, specific, a point of view, no guru phrasing, no dashes. Opens with the claim, not "I". Under 200 words. One image direction.
- Approval `social.post`. Never posted without his yes.

**Quarterly**: one op-ed pitch to trade or business press (Campaign India, afaqs, Storyboard18, Campaign Middle East), one speaking or podcast pitch. Targets: 2 to 3 op-eds and 1 to 2 talks a year.

**Track**: posts, reach, inbound DMs that turned into leads (os-responder tags source 'founder').
