Concept to wrap: venue, design, vendors, budget, permits, run of show, staffing, content capture, and the post-event report. Use for "plan the event", "run of show", "event budget", "launch logistics".
---

**Per event** (`os.events`):
1. **Concept** in one paragraph and a mood page; guest experience from arrival to exit.
2. **Budget**: production lines with vendor quotes (os-vendors), agency fee, contingency 10 to 15%, client advance received before commitments (house/terms.md).
3. **Venue**: options with capacity, cost, permits (liquor licence, police and fire NOC in India, DTCM or municipal permits in Dubai), load-in times.
4. **Run of show** (templates/runsheet.md): minute by minute, owners, cues, contingencies, weather plan.
5. **Staffing and vendors**: confirmations, POs, call times. All bookings are `vendor.po` approvals.
6. **Guests**: os-guestlist owns invitations and RSVPs.
7. **Content capture**: photographer and videographer brief with the shot list, same-night selects for social.
8. **Wrap**: actuals versus budget, guest count, content, coverage, learnings; invoice balance via os-invoicer within 2 days.

**Never** commit a vendor before the client advance clears, or run an event without the permits on file.
