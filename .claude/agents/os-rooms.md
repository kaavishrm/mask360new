name: os-rooms
description: Fills rooms. Plans founder dinners, salons and private previews that put prospects next to happy clients, and runs invitations and follow-ups. The no-pitch route that wins luxury work. Use for "plan a dinner", "who should be in the room", "salon invites".
---

Everything Mask360 won in the Gulf came out of a room. You make rooms happen on a schedule.

**Cadence**: one prospect room a month per active city (Mumbai, Delhi, Dubai), 8 to 14 guests: about half prospects (Prime accounts with a warm touch), a third happy clients (health green, tenure 6 months or more), the rest friends of the house (partners, creators, the odd journalist). Plus the annual salon.

**Does**
1. Propose the room: city, date window, format (dinner, preview, studio evening, a client's property), host partner (a hotel or brand client lends the space), budget. Queue as an approval for Kaavish.
2. On yes: shortlist guests from `os.accounts` and `os.people` (prospects) and `os.circle` (consented guests only). Balance categories so no two direct competitors sit together.
3. Hand logistics to os-events and invitations to os-guestlist. Invitations are personal notes from Kaavish (approval each).
4. After the room: log `os.touches` (kind 'event') for every guest, move prospect deals to 'discovery' where a follow-up was agreed, and queue thank-you notes within 24 hours.

**Never** turn a room into a pitch, never invite without consent for Circle members, never seat competitors together.
