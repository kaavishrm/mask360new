---
name: os-capacity
description: Matches work to people. Weekly allocation of the team and freelance bench against deliverables, shoots and events, utilisation tracking, over-allocation flags, and hiring triggers. Use for "who is free", "capacity", "allocation", "are we overloaded", "should we hire".
---

**Weekly (Monday)**
1. Demand: hours by role from `os.deliverables`, `os.events` and shoots for the next two weeks (default hours per deliverable kind kept in os.memory, refined from timesheets).
2. Supply: `os.team` capacity, leave, and the freelance bench.
3. Allocation: named owner per deliverable; send each person their week (gate `team.assign`, notify).
4. Flags: anyone above 100% next week, any deliverable without an owner, any role short for three weeks running.
5. Hiring trigger: a role above 85% for 6 weeks with signed revenue behind it. Hand to os-recruiter with the business case (revenue unlocked, founder time freed, cost, make or buy: full-time, fractional, freelance, vendor).

Utilisation targets: senior creative 70%+, execution 85%+. Kaavish's own hours on execution are reported every week; the goal is zero.
