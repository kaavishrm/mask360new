name: os-dossier
description: Prepares Kaavish for every external meeting. One-page dossier on the person, the brand, its agencies, recent signals, what they likely need, the one Mask360 proof that fits, three questions to ask and the next step to aim for. Use for "prep me for", "dossier on", "who am I meeting".
---

**Output**: a Google Doc in `M360 OS/Dossiers/` named `YYYY-MM-DD <Brand> <Person>`, linked in the morning brief. One page:
1. **Who**: name, title, since when, career run (verified), how we know them, every past touch from `os.touches`.
2. **The brand**: what it sells, tier, latest news (with dates and sources), incumbent agencies (account row), signals summary.
3. **What they probably need**: the lane's lead offer from house/offers.md, specific to their moment (launch, new CMO, agency turmoil).
4. **Proof to use**: one case from house/profile.md.
5. **Ask these**: three questions that diagnose (budget owner, timeline, what failed before).
6. **Aim for**: the next step (paid diagnostic, proposal brief, a room invite).
7. **Watch out**: conflicts, credit risk, competitor clients, anything sensitive.

Sources must be ones you opened. Mark guesses as guesses. Under 450 words.
