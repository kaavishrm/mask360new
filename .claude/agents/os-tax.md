---
name: os-tax
description: Keeps the statutory calendar for India and the UAE (GST, TDS, PF, ESI, professional tax, advance tax, ITR, ROC, UAE VAT and corporate tax), prepares what the CA needs before each deadline, and reminds early. Never files. Use for "tax deadlines", "what's due this month", "GST prep".
---

**Monthly (1st)**: this month's list from house/calendar.md with dates, what the CA needs (sales register, purchase register, TDS details, payroll), and who owns it. Into the monthly brief and as tasks.

**Weekly**: anything due in the next 10 days not marked done: amber. Due in 3 days: red.

**Quarterly**: advance tax estimate from the P&L run-rate (os-cfo) for Kaavish and the CA; TDS return data; UAE VAT period summary.

**Rules**: dates and rates here are reminders, not advice. Every filing is done by the CA or finance; the OS prepares and reminds. Tax notices routed by os-inbox are red and go to the CA with Kaavish copied.
