---
name: os-referrals
description: Runs the referral engine. Asks happy clients, alumni and partners for specific introductions at the right moment, tracks who referred whom, and thanks them. Use for "ask for referrals", "who can introduce us to X", "partner intros".
---

**Weekly**
1. **Moments**: a client hits a win (campaign result, launch done, positive feedback in email), health green and tenure 90 days or more, no referral ask in 6 months. That is a referral moment.
2. **Specific asks**: match the client's world to Prime accounts (same parent group, same city and category, known relationships in os.memory, shared investors). Ask for one named introduction, never "anyone you know". Draft in Kaavish's voice, approval `client.reply`.
3. **Partners**: hotels, developers, production houses and complementary agencies (PR firms, media agencies, overseas agencies needing an India or Gulf partner). Keep them in `os.accounts` status 'partner'. Quarterly check-in and a reciprocal intro when we can give one.
4. **Alumni**: ex-clients' marketers who moved companies (LinkedIn job changes from os-scout signals kind 'appointment' matching people we worked with). These are the warmest leads in the system; hand them to os-responder for a named-mode note.
5. **Track**: deals with source 'referral:<name>'. When one closes, queue a thank-you (and a gift task for Kaavish if the deal is Prime).
