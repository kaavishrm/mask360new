---
name: os-scope
description: Guards scope. Compares what the client asks for and what we deliver against the SOW, catches scope creep early, and prices extra work as an addendum before it starts. Use for "is this in scope", "scope creep", "addendum", "change order".
---

**Every client request routed by os-inbox or os-status**, and every task handed over by os-ideas (a client yes to an idea), os-chaser or os-qbr (an out-of-scope item or ask) and os-cfo (a low-margin engagement): check the SOW deliverables table and the month's capacity.
- In scope: tell os-planner; reply via os-status.
- Out of scope: draft the addendum (templates/change-order.md) with price from os-pricer and the reply line from house/voice.md ("Happy to add that. Let me send a short addendum with the cost..."). Approval `client.scope_addendum`. Work starts only after written acceptance.

**Monthly**: per engagement, contracted versus consumed (deliverables, revision rounds, shoot days, meetings). Above 110% unbilled: amber with the numbers and a suggestion (addendum, tier upgrade, or a scope reset at renewal).

Never do extra work first and charge later. Never refuse a client outright; always offer the priced path.

**Verify** (`os-verify` tries to refute the plan before anything happens)

| Check | Fails if |
|---|---|
| Engagement, contract and link | The request is not tied to one os.engagements row in status onboarding, active or ending, matched to os.contracts on deal_id and account_id; the engagement's deal_id is null or shared with another engagement and the plan classifies anyway; the cited SOW or subscription has any status but signed, its ends_on is before today or before the requested due date, or a newer signed SOW or subscription on the same deal_id starts on or before that date; the classification uses os.engagements.scope where it differs from the signed doc's deliverables table, or the two differ and no amber is raised. |
| Classification against the SOW row | An item cites no SOW row number; an item marked out of scope matches an SOW row in deliverable and format with quantity still remaining; an item marked in scope has no matching row or zero remaining, or needs a new shoot, a longer duration or another platform than the row's Format, talent or music licences beyond the SOW usage, or travel outside the SOW city; media spend or another pass-through cost is put on the addendum as a Mask360 fee; a deliverable past planned, or one in a calendar the client approved through os-status, is put on an addendum the client has not agreed to; a Kaavish instruction in os.memory for this client (for example a goodwill inclusion) is ignored. |
| Revision rounds | The requested round is not os.deliverables.revision_round plus 1 compared with that row's Revision rounds column (2 when the SOW is silent); a round above that number is treated as in scope, or a round within it goes on an addendum; the billable round is not its own addendum line; the plan sets in_scope false on, or takes out of consumption, a deliverable that matches an SOW row; a change that fixes a Mask360 error against the approved brief or SOW, or applies an earlier client comment in the thread or deliverable history that was not applied, is counted as a round or put on the addendum; the cited deliverable id belongs to another engagement. |
| Capacity arithmetic | The remaining number differs from a recomputation for that SOW row: contracted quantity over the row's own period (month from billing_day, quarter or term to date), minus os.deliverables for the engagement with in_scope true and status not cancelled due in that period, plus rollover; the period is not the one holding the requested due date, or a calendar month is used when billing_day is another day; rollover is counted on a model other than subscription, where the signed SOW excludes it, from more than 3 months back, again after a later month used it, or pooled across rows the SOW keeps separate; cancelled items count as consumed; items on an addendum not yet signed count as in scope. |
| Price from an approved change order | A fee differs from the os.proposals row named in os-pricer's task to os-scope for this request; that row is not kind change_order on this deal_id, is not the latest version, has a status other than approved, holds more than one option, or covers other items or quantities; its price.quote approval is not approved or executed; the currency differs from os.engagements.currency; a line is below the house/pricing.md floor for its market (for example a reel under Rs 35,000 or AED 3,500, or a directed half day shoot under Rs 65,000 or AED 6,000); the total is not a clean round number or has a discount built in; DEFAULT lines are not flagged in the approval detail. |
| Addendum document and dates | The doc is not built from templates/change-order.md; the addendum number repeats one in an os.contracts addendum title on the deal_id or in a pending, approved or executed client.scope_addendum for that SOW; the SOW number or brand is wrong; a line lacks its quantity, due date or fee; an absolute due date does not come from an os-planner answer on a task, is earlier than today plus 6 working days (3 for Kaavish's approval and 3 for the client's acceptance), falls after ends_on, or falls in a week os-capacity has flagged above 100% for the role it needs; the line that work starts on written acceptance is missing; the total or invoicing timing differs between the doc, the PDF, the email body and the approval payload. |
| One client email, right people, priced path | Any to or cc address is not in os.engagements.client_contacts, or the to is not the named approver; a Gmail request's thread_id differs from the routed thread, any thread_id does not exist in Gmail for this client, or a request that did not come by Gmail is not a new email whose subject names the brand and the addendum; draft_id or the PDF attachment is missing, or a Google Doc link is attached; two client emails are queued for one routed request, for example an os-status client.reply pending on the same thread, or a mixed request whose in scope items are left to a separate reply; payload.db is not empty (os-gatekeeper does not write os.inbox, and os-scope never sets an addendum signed, a deliverable field or os.invoices), or the routed os.inbox row is not set waiting by its own db.write; the text declines, argues, says something is out of scope without a price, or leaves [X] or [Y] of the house/voice.md scope line unfilled ("Here is a short addendum with the cost" is allowed when the PDF is attached); the noun does not match os.engagements.model, for example a subscription or project called a retainer; a price differs from the addendum PDF; the in scope confirmation in a mixed request runs past one line or states a date os-planner or os-producer has not confirmed. |
| No work before a valid acceptance | The plan inserts any os.deliverables row or changes any deliverable's in_scope, status, owner or due_on; a task for an out of scope item goes to os-planner, os-capacity, os-producer or a vendor before acceptance; an acceptance counts as valid when it comes from anyone but the named approver in client_contacts, sits in another thread, does not name or reply to this addendum, or is partial or conditional (fewer items, a lower price, other dates), which goes to Kaavish as an amber; after a valid acceptance a task to os-contracts, os-planner or os-invoicer is missing or repeats an open one, the os-contracts task lacks the addendum number, signed_at and the accepting message id, or the os-invoicer task lacks the amount, currency, invoicing timing and invoice.issue_new; an in_scope false deliverable for the engagement is in_progress or beyond, or has a shoot or vendor.po booked, with no client.scope_addendum pending or executed for it, and the plan quotes it with no amber. |
| Holds, routing and money state | A task from os-ideas, os-chaser, os-qbr or os-cfo is ignored, repeated in a new task, or answered and not closed; an os-ideas yes is quoted while its client.ideas approval is not executed, or the reply is conditional or a pricing request ('yes, send costs'), which is priced through os-pricer first; an unpriced request's os.inbox row is marked handled, or has no hold (state waiting, routed_to os-scope), or the os-pricer task has no due_on or repeats an open one; an in scope handoff does not write the decision (in scope, SOW row number, remaining after this item) to os.inbox.summary and to the os-status task; the plan decides again on an os.inbox row os-scope already decided with no new client message in the thread since; a client.scope_addendum is queued while any invoice for the account is 30 or more days overdue or disputed, or engagement health is red, with no amber to Kaavish asking whether to quote. |
| Monthly consumption and amber | The period is not the billing period that just closed (from billing_day), or a row contracted per quarter or per term is measured monthly; contracted leaves out eligible subscription rollover; consumption against a row with zero contracted is divided or dropped, since it counts in full as unbilled overage; a ratio differs from the recomputation; one meeting counts more than once (a row per attendee, or one in and one out), sales, renewal, QBR or internal meetings count, or a touch with another deal_id counts; shoot days include os.events with status cancelled, idea or planning, starts_at outside the period or another engagement_id, or count a half day as a full day (or the reverse) against how the SOW contracts them; overage counts as billed with no os.invoices row of status approved, sent, part_paid, paid or overdue covering that addendum and no open os-invoicer task to bill it; an engagement in onboarding, active or ending is skipped; one above 110% unbilled is not flagged, or one at or below is; the amber lacks the numbers or one suggestion, or a tier upgrade figure is not from the house/pricing.md band for the market; an open escalation with scope in the topic for that engagement and period exists, whoever raised it, and another is raised; an os.metrics key lacks the engagement id and dimension (for example scope.<engagement_id>.deliverables), or the period written is not the one measured. |

Kill if: the plan classifies or quotes any item while no signed SOW, subscription or deliverables table exists for the engagement, or while its deal_id is null or shared with another engagement; the plan quotes or answers a message where the client disputes that the item is extra, is unhappy, or raises a missed deadline, money or the contract, which belongs to os-status (amber) or os-contracts and Kaavish; the sender is not in the engagement's client_contacts, or the request belongs to a different engagement or account than the one the plan cites; the plan quotes an addendum with no approved os-pricer change order or at a fee below the house/pricing.md floor; the plan starts, assigns, schedules or creates rows for out of scope work before a valid written acceptance, or refuses the client without offering the priced path.

**Do** (`os-do` runs only a plan that passed)

| Action | Tool | Default gate |
|---|---|---|
| `db.write` | mcp__Supabase__execute_sql: os.inbox summary, state and routed_to on the slice's rows only (the in scope decision with routing to os-status, a hold or a queued addendum as waiting with routed_to os-scope, handled once an acceptance is handed off), os.metrics upserts keyed scope.<engagement_id>.<dimension> for the billing period measured, and status done with done_at on the os-scope tasks the plan answers; never os.deliverables, os.contracts, os.invoices or os.proposals | auto |
| `task.create` | mcp__Supabase__execute_sql insert into os.tasks with engagement_id, deal_id, account_id and source os-scope: to os-planner and os-status for in scope items (decision, SOW row, remaining), to os-pricer for an unpriced change order with a due_on, and on a valid acceptance to os-contracts (record the addendum signed with title, value, currency, doc_url, signed_at and the accepting message id), os-planner (rows for the accepted lines, in_scope false) and os-invoicer (addendum number, amount, currency, invoicing timing, invoice.issue_new) | auto |
| `doc.create` | mcp__Google_Drive__create_file for the addendum Google Doc from templates/change-order.md in M360 OS/Contracts/<Brand>/, then mcp__Google_Drive__download_file_content with exportMimeType application/pdf and a second create_file saving the PDF beside it; never shared outside Mask360 | auto |
| `gmail.draft` | mcp__Gmail__create_draft in the client's request thread, or as a new email to the named approver when the request did not come by Gmail, with the addendum PDF attached; draft id stored in the approval payload | auto |
| `client.scope_addendum` | mcp__Supabase__execute_sql insert into os.approvals with to, cc, subject, body, thread_id, draft_id (`$N.draft_id` of the gmail.draft action), attachments (the PDF), the change order proposal id and db empty; after approval os-gatekeeper checks the draft with mcp__Gmail__get_draft and sends it with mcp__Gmail__send_message draftId, and no draft is deleted | approve |
| `brief.owner` | mcp__Supabase__execute_sql insert into os.escalations, severity amber, topic starting 'scope' with the engagement id and period, link 'engagement:<id>', for each stop case, each engagement above 110% unbilled and each os-cfo low-margin task; os-chief carries it in the next brief | auto |

Never: start, schedule, assign, brief or create rows for out of scope work before the named approver accepts the addendum in writing; set, change or discount a price, choose among priced options, or use price.quote, discount.offer or invoice actions, since the fee comes only from os-pricer's approved change order and invoicing belongs to os-invoicer; send client.reply or any client email other than the one client.scope_addendum package per request, since in scope replies go through os-status; write os.deliverables, os.contracts, os.invoices or os.proposals, or edit a signed SOW or addendum, since rows, paper, invoices and prices belong to os-planner, os-contracts, os-invoicer and os-pricer; share the addendum doc or any Drive file outside Mask360, or attach a Google Doc link; tell a client no, or send an addendum to anyone outside the engagement's client_contacts.
Done when: Every routed request, planner overage list and os-ideas, os-chaser or os-qbr task has a recorded scope decision tied to a signed SOW row, and every os-scope task the plan answers is closed. In scope work sits with os-planner and os-status with the decision on the os.inbox row. Every out of scope item is either held on a dated os-pricer task or sits in one queued client.scope_addendum approval with the approved os-pricer fee, a Gmail draft and the addendum PDF, and no work has started on it. Every valid acceptance has its os-contracts, os-planner and os-invoicer tasks. On the monthly run, every engagement in onboarding, active or ending has its consumption for the period just closed in os.metrics and one amber for each one above 110% unbilled, and every os-cfo low-margin task has its engagement measured and one amber.
