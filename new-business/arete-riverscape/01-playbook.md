# Playbook: how we sell Riverscape

Arete already ran ads, email and channel partners. They did not work, because nobody buys a project hit by the July flood at the old price and the old terms. We do three things differently: change the offer, sell in blocks, and close at the site.

## The offer (week 1, Arete approves)

- 20 villas relaunched with the post-flood redesign as the news.
- Launch price for those 20, valid 45 days, then it rises. The deadline is real.
- Flood cover in the agreement: Arete pays each villa's home insurance, flood included, for 5 years from possession (the policy has to be in the owner's name), and repairs flood damage to common areas. Estimated Rs 3.7 to 4.6 lakh for 20 villas over 5 years before any post-flood loading. Get broker quotes in week 1. See `research/insurance.md`.
- 10:90 payment plan: 10% at booking, 90% at possession.
- Buyer referral reward for existing Riverscape buyers.

## 1. Corporate deals

**Owner:** corporate manager. **Meetings:** Kaavish and Arete's sales head.
**Goal:** 60 companies worked, 12 meetings held, 3 to 4 serious deals, 1 to 2 signed blocks.

### Step 1: long list of 60 (days 1 to 5)
- Start from `research/corporate-targets.csv`.
- Add Arete's own list: tenants, suppliers and partners of their nine industrial parks, plus 20 names the promoter knows personally.
- Rank by: plant size, senior staff from outside the region, Mumbai or Surat promoter family, recent expansion.

### Step 2: three named people per company (days 3 to 7)
- HR or admin head: handles director housing and guest houses. First yes.
- CFO or finance head: approves buying or leasing.
- MD, promoter or plant head: often buys personally, which is our second sale.
- Tools: Apollo for names, emails, phones (enrichment uses credits, approve the spend first), LinkedIn to verify.

### Step 3: get meetings, warm routes first (day 7 onward)
1. **Promoter introductions.** Arete's promoter sends our one-line ask to their 20 contacts (`03-scripts-corporate.md`, script C1). Expect most early meetings from here.
2. **Associations.** Arete asks one association (see `research/associations.md`) for a 20 minute slot at a monthly meeting, or co-hosts an industrialists' evening at the experience centre.
3. **Direct outreach, phone first.** Per company, over 10 days:

| Day | Action | Script |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Call the MD's or HR head's assistant, in Gujarati. Email the one-page note from Arete's sales head. | C2, C3 |
| 3 | LinkedIn connection to HR head and CFO, with a short note. | C4 |
| 5 | Second call. Offer two specific meeting slots. | C2 |
| 8 | Hand-deliver the box to the plant office: promoter's invitation, brochure, proof pack. | C5 |
| 10 | Final call. No response: park it, move to the next company. | C2 |

**Daily target, corporate manager:** 25 calls, 10 emails, 10 LinkedIn messages. Logged in the tracker the same day.

### Step 4: the meeting (45 minutes, their office)
Agenda in `03-scripts-corporate.md`, C6. Key points:
- Their housing problem first: where directors and senior staff stay now, what it costs, how often guests visit.
- Two options: buy a block, or lease from an investor owner. Many companies prefer to lease, because a company-owned home used by a director can create a perquisite tax charge for the director.
- The flood proof pack, the term sheet, and an invitation for the MD's family to the next Open Weekend.
- Leave the term sheet, fix a follow-up date. Arete's legal team drafts the agreement.

## 2. Wealth manager network

**Owner:** adviser manager. Kaavish joins the first 10 conversations.
**Goal:** 150 called, 60 met, 25 signed, each bringing 3 to 5 families to one Open Weekend.

### Who
- Target: independent wealth managers, mutual fund distributors, boutique family offices, CAs serving business families.
- Do not target: bank private banking relationship managers (their employers usually bar personal referral fees) and SEBI-registered investment advisers (SEBI bars them from referral fees). A bank can only be a head-office tie-up, which takes months.
- Check before paying CAs: a fee to a practising CA for a referral may conflict with the ICAI Code of Ethics. Take advice before offering fees to the CA firms on the list. CAs can still introduce clients unpaid.
- Start with the A and B rows in `research/adviser-targets.csv`. Confirm every distributor's ARN on AMFI before onboarding. Many surnames from Apollo are masked: check LinkedIn, or approve Apollo enrichment credits.

### Steps
1. **List (days 1 to 7):** start from `research/adviser-targets.csv`. Extend with AMFI's distributor search, LinkedIn, ICAI branches and BNI-type chapters (method in `research/adviser-targets-notes.md`).
2. **Recruit (weeks 2 to 5):** call, then a coffee meeting. Script in `04-scripts-advisers.md`.
3. **Sign up:** 2-page referral agreement, 30 minute briefing on the investor lot note, an adviser site day before they bring clients.
4. **Introductions:** adviser registers each family by WhatsApp to the desk (name, phone, adviser). Desk replies with a timestamped confirmation. Credit lasts 90 days from registration.
5. **Payout:** 3 to 4% of agreement value, under a written agreement. Under the RERA Act anyone paid for introducing buyers is an agent, so **every adviser must hold their own GujRERA agent registration before any fee is paid** (Form G, Rs 10,000 for an individual, Rs 50,000 for a firm, valid 5 years). We help each adviser file in their first week. An adviser who will not register can still introduce, but cannot be paid. Mask360 registers too, for the success fee. See `research/gujrera-agent-registration.md`.

## 3. Open Weekends

**Owner:** sales director. Full run sheet in `06-open-weekends.md`.
**Goal:** 6 weekends, 25 families each, bookings taken on the spot.
- The single thing that decides bookings: someone from Arete who can approve a price on the spot is present at every weekend.

## 4. Follow-up desk

**Owner:** 2 desk executives. Templates in `05-scripts-follow-up.md`.
- Arete's WhatsApp Business number on a shared inbox. One tracker.
- Statuses: introduced, confirmed, visited, price discussed, booked, lost (with reason).
- Every visitor gets a message within 24 hours: thank you, proof pack, the exact price discussed. Then a call on day 3, a message on day 7, a call on day 14. Then lost, with a reason.

## Weekly rhythm

- **Daily 9:30 am standup, 15 minutes:** yesterday's calls, meetings booked, families confirmed for the next weekend.
- **Monday:** one-page report to Arete: meetings, families, visits, bookings, by channel and by adviser. Lost reasons.
