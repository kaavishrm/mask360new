# Follow-up desk templates

For the desk executives, on Arete's WhatsApp Business number. Send only to people who were introduced, replied, visited or asked. Never to bought lists. Switch to Gujarati or Hindi if the family prefers. If anyone says stop, remove them the same day and log it.

Before going live: WhatsApp Business templates used to start a conversation must be approved by Meta. Submit F1, F2 and F3 in week 1.

## F1. Open Weekend invitation (after an introduction)

> Hello [Name], this is [desk name] from Riverscape by Arete Group. [Adviser or company contact name] mentioned you may like to see Riverscape, our riverfront villas in Tukwada, Vapi. We are hosting a few families on [Saturday date] and [Sunday date], with a car from [Mumbai / Surat], a walk through the site with our engineer, and lunch. Would either day suit you? Reply 1 for Saturday or 2 for Sunday.

## F2. Confirmation (5 days before)

> Thank you, [Name]. You are confirmed for [day, date]. Your car will pick you up at [time] from [address]. Driver: [name, number], shared on the day. Arrival at Riverscape around [time], lunch at [time], back by [time]. How many of you will come, and any food preferences?

## F3. Day-before reminder

> Hi [Name], looking forward to seeing you tomorrow. Pickup at [time] from [address]. If anything changes, message me here. Location pin: [link].

## F4. 24 hours after the visit

> Thank you for visiting Riverscape yesterday, [Name]. As promised, here is the proof pack with the site levels and the engineer's letter. The price we discussed for [villa type, unit] was Rs [amount] at the launch price, valid until [date]. The payment plan is 10% at booking and 90% at possession. Happy to answer anything, or set up a call with [sales head name].

## F5. Day 3 call (script)

> Hi [Name], it is [desk name] from Riverscape. Did you get a chance to look at the proof pack? Any questions from the family? (Listen, note objections.) Shall I hold [unit] for you until [date]? Would you like a call with [sales head name]?

## F6. Day 7 message

> Hi [Name], a quick update: [x] of the 20 launch-price villas are now booked. [Unit] is still available at Rs [amount] until [date]. If it helps, we can arrange a second visit on a weekday for the family.

## F7. Day 14 call, then close the loop

Ask directly: are they still considering it, and what is stopping them? Log the reason exactly: price, flood worry, location, timing, possession date, family not agreed, bought elsewhere, other. Then mark as lost with the reason, or set a new date if they asked for one.

## F8. Booking confirmation (after a booking)

> Congratulations and thank you, [Name]. Your booking for [unit] at Riverscape is confirmed, booking amount Rs [amount] received on [date]. [Sales head name] will share the agreement within [x] days. Welcome to Riverscape.

## Tracker statuses

`introduced` > `confirmed` > `visited` > `price discussed` > `booked` or `lost (reason)`

Update the status the same day. Every row must have a next step and a date, unless booked or lost.
