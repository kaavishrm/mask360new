# Corporate scripts

For the corporate manager, Kaavish and Arete's sales head. Fill the [brackets]. Gujarati lines need a native speaker's check before first use. Prices only from Arete's approved price sheet.

## C1. Promoter introduction (Arete's promoter sends, WhatsApp or SMS, personal)

**English**
> [First name], hope all is well. We have relaunched 20 villas at Riverscape, our riverfront project in Tukwada, after redesigning the site following the July rains. A few companies in the belt are taking villas for their directors and guest houses. Would you have 30 minutes next week for my team? [Kaavish / sales head name] will call your office. Regards, [Promoter name]

**Gujarati**
> [નામ], આશા છે કે બધું બરાબર છે. અમે તુકવાડાના અમારા Riverscape પ્રોજેક્ટમાં, જુલાઈના વરસાદ પછી સાઇટ ફરીથી ડિઝાઇન કરીને, 20 વિલા ફરી લોન્ચ કર્યા છે. આ વિસ્તારની કેટલીક કંપનીઓ તેમના ડિરેક્ટરો અને ગેસ્ટ હાઉસ માટે વિલા લઈ રહી છે. શું આવતા અઠવાડિયે મારી ટીમ માટે 30 મિનિટ મળી શકે? [નામ] તમારી ઓફિસમાં ફોન કરશે. [પ્રમોટરનું નામ]

## C2. Phone call to the assistant (corporate manager)

**Opening (Gujarati first, switch to Hindi or English if they do)**
> નમસ્તે, હું Arete Group તરફથી [તમારું નામ] બોલું છું. [MD / HR head name] સાહેબ સાથે 30 મિનિટની મીટિંગ માટે ફોન કર્યો છે. [Promoter name] સાહેબે તેમની સાથે વાત કરી છે.

**English version**
> Good morning, this is [your name] from Arete Group. I am calling to set up a 30 minute meeting with [name]. [Promoter name] has spoken to him about it. (Use the last line only if C1 was sent.)

**If asked "what is it about?"**
> Arete has relaunched 20 villas at Riverscape in Tukwada, ten minutes from your plant. Several companies in the belt use villas for directors, senior staff and visiting guests, either bought or leased. We would like to show [name] the options. It takes 30 minutes at your office.

**If asked to send an email**
> Of course. I will send a one-page note today. Can I call you on [day] to fix the time? Which is the best email?

**If "not interested" or "we don't need housing"**
> Understood. Do senior people from Mumbai or abroad visit the plant? Where do they stay today? (Listen. If hotels, offer the guest house angle. If still no, thank them, log as "no need", move on.)

**Always close with two slots**
> Would Tuesday at 11 or Thursday at 4 be better for [name]?

**Log in the tracker:** date, who you spoke to, outcome, next step and date.

## C6. The meeting (45 minutes, their office)

**Attendees:** Kaavish or the sales director, Arete's sales head. Bring the corporate deck, the proof pack, the term sheet, a price sheet for the 20 relaunch villas.

| Minutes | What |
|---|---|
| 0 to 10 | Their situation. Where do directors and senior staff live now? What does it cost? How many visitors a month, where do they stay? Any staff relocated from outside Gujarat? |
| 10 to 20 | Riverscape in brief: location, villa types, the redesign and the proof pack. Hand over the engineer's letter. |
| 20 to 30 | Two options. **Buy a block** of 3 to 10 at the relaunch price, 10:90 plan. **Lease** from investor owners, company pays rent only. Explain why many prefer lease (no capital, no perquisite tax issue for directors). |
| 30 to 40 | Their questions. Possession date, maintenance, security, distance from the plant. |
| 40 to 45 | Next step: term sheet review by their finance or legal, a date for the follow-up call, and an invitation for the MD's family to the next Open Weekend. |

**After the meeting, same day:** thank-you email with the term sheet, tracker updated, follow-up date set.

**Questions to be ready for:**
- *Is the site safe now?* Show the July water level against the new finished levels, the second road, drainage, the engineer's letter. Never say flood-proof or guaranteed.
- *What does it cost to lease?* Only quote what Arete and the investor have agreed. If not yet agreed, say you will confirm in writing within 48 hours.
- *Can we see it?* Offer a weekday site visit for the HR head, or the next Open Weekend.
