## What that means

- **The fixed fee only covers the cost.** Rs 12,00,000 in, about Rs 12,10,000 out. All profit comes from the success fee.
- **Downside:** if fewer than 3 villas are booked by day 60, we waive the Rs 3,00,000 day 60 instalment. Worst case is a loss of about Rs 3,10,000 plus internal team time.
- **Success share:** 30% of each success fee goes to the sales team pool (`02-team-and-hiring.md`). Mask360 keeps 70%.

Net after costs = fixed fee (minus Rs 3,00,000 if the waiver applies) + Mask360's 70% of the success fee, minus costs of Rs 12,10,000.

## Levers if the numbers need protecting
1. Hire the sales director on a lower fixed fee with a higher success share.
2. Move the film shoot to Arete's existing footage plus one shoot day.
3. Ask for the Open Weekend budget and all travel at actuals, which the proposal already says.
4. Do not extend the 90 days without a new fixed fee.
