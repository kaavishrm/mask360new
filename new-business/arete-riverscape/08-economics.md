## What that means

- **The fixed fee now covers cost with a margin.** Rs 18,00,000 in, about Rs 15,60,000 out: about Rs 2,40,000 before any villa sells.
- **Downside:** if fewer than 3 villas are booked by day 60, we waive the Rs 4,50,000 day 60 instalment. With no sales at all, the loss is about Rs 2,10,000.
- **Success share:** 30% of each success fee goes to the sales team pool (`02-team-and-hiring.md`). Mask360 keeps 70%.

## Levers if the numbers need protecting
1. Hire the sales director on a lower fixed fee with a higher success share.
2. Use Arete's existing footage plus one shoot day for the film.
3. Keep Open Weekends, media and travel at actuals, as the proposal says.
4. Do not extend the 90 days without a new fixed fee.
