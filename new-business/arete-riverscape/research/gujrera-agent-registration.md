- **UNVERIFIED:** blogs, finance portals, or my reading. Confirm on the official portal.

## Bottom line

1. Mask360 (company) can register as a GujRERA real estate agent: Form G, fee Rs 50,000 (individual Rs 10,000), valid 5 years, renewal Rs 25,000 (individual Rs 5,000) applied at least 3 months before expiry. VERIFIED-SECONDARY (Gujarat RERA General Rules 2017, rules 10 to 12).
2. Anyone who introduces buyers for a fee is a "real estate agent" under the Act and needs their own registration to facilitate sales in a registered project. An unregistered wealth manager should not be paid a referral fee. Penalty exposure: Rs 10,000 per day of default. (Act sections 2(zm), 9, 62; VERIFIED-SECONDARY.)
3. SEBI-registered investment advisers (RIAs) are a poor fit for paid referrals: SEBI rules bar them from taking commissions or referral fees for implementation, and require advisory and distribution clients to be kept separate. Real estate sits outside SEBI's purview, so the rule's direct reach to a property referral is not settled, but the conflict-of-interest risk is high. Recruit non-RIA wealth professionals who register with GujRERA, or pay nothing.

## 1. Who must register

- **Definition (RERA Act 2016, section 2(zm)):** a real estate agent is any person who negotiates or acts on behalf of one person in a transfer of a plot, apartment or building in a real estate project, by way of sale, with another person, and receives remuneration or fees or any other charges, whether as commission or otherwise, **and includes a person who introduces, through any medium, prospective buyers and sellers to each other for negotiation**, and includes property dealers, brokers and middlemen. VERIFIED-SECONDARY (Lexology, Mondaq, ibclaw summaries; wording widely quoted).
- **Section 9(1):** no real estate agent shall facilitate the sale or purchase of, or act on behalf of any person to facilitate the sale or purchase of, any plot, apartment or building in a registered real estate project in the planning area without obtaining registration. VERIFIED-SECONDARY.
- **Which authority:** the authority of the state where the project sits. Riverscape is in Vapi, Gujarat, so GujRERA. A Mumbai-based referrer selling a Gujarat project still needs GujRERA, not (only) MahaRERA. UNVERIFIED reading of section 9 plus rules; standard practice.
- **Single registration covers the whole state** (per third-party portal guide). UNVERIFIED.

## 2. Eligibility

- Individuals, proprietorships, partnership firms, LLPs and companies may apply. Rule 10 asks for "personal or partner details" and enterprise details. VERIFIED-SECONDARY.
- No minimum qualification or competency exam found in the Gujarat Rules text reviewed. MahaRERA requires a training certificate; whether GujRERA has added a similar requirement after 2017 was NOT FOUND. Check before filing.
- Practical prerequisite (portal guide): create an "Agent Enrollment" login on the GujRERA portal first, then apply from the Agent Dashboard. UNVERIFIED.

## 5. Timeline

- **Grant or reject:** section 9 requires the authority to grant or reject within the period prescribed; if the applicant hears nothing within that period, it is deemed registered. Secondary sources state the period as **30 days** (section 9(4) read with the rules). VERIFIED-SECONDARY for the principle; the exact Gujarat period was NOT confirmed on an official source.
- **Rejection only after a hearing** (rule 11 and 12 references to Form I and opportunity to be heard). VERIFIED-SECONDARY.
- **Practical plan:** budget 30 to 45 days from complete filing. If Mask360 needs to be paid on Riverscape sales inside the 90-day mandate, file in week 1.

## 6. Validity and renewal

- Registration valid **5 years** (rule 11). Certificate issued in **Form H**; rejection in **Form I**. VERIFIED-SECONDARY.
- Renewal in **Form J**, filed **not less than 3 months before expiry**, with updated rule 10 documents; renewal granted in **Form K**, valid 5 years (rule 12). VERIFIED-SECONDARY.
- Revocation possible under section 9(7) (misrepresentation, fraud, breach of conditions, Act or rules) after a hearing (rule 13). VERIFIED-SECONDARY.

## 7. Obligations once registered

Section 10 of the Act (VERIFIED-SECONDARY, ibclaw and taxguru reproductions; lettering differs across sources):
- Not facilitate the sale or purchase of any unit in a project that is not registered with RERA. Riverscape's GujRERA number must be checked and quoted.
- **Maintain and preserve books of account, records and documents** as prescribed. Gujarat rule 14: per the Income Tax Act 1961 and its rules.
- **Not engage in unfair trade practices**, including: false representation of the standard, quality or grade of services; representing that the promoter or agent has an approval or affiliation it does not have; false or misleading representations about services; permitting publication of any advertisement for services that are not intended to be offered.
- Facilitate the allottee's possession of all documents they are entitled to at the time of booking.
- Discharge other functions prescribed. Gujarat rule 15: assist the allottee and promoter to exercise their rights and fulfil their obligations at booking and sale.
- Quote the agent registration number on every sale facilitated and in marketing (portal guide; standard practice). UNVERIFIED for Gujarat-specific wording.
- **Mask360 implication:** every ad, WhatsApp creative and brochure produced for the mandate should carry Riverscape's project RERA number and Mask360's agent number once issued. The flood relaunch messaging (insurance offer, "flood-safe" claims) must be accurate or it is an unfair trade practice.

## 8. Penalty

- **Section 62:** failure to register under section 9 or contravention of section 10 attracts a penalty of **Rs 10,000 for every day** the default continues, which may cumulatively extend up to **5% of the cost** of the plot, apartment or building whose sale was facilitated. Rs 10,000 per day: VERIFIED-SECONDARY. 5% cap: from the Act as commonly quoted; NOT confirmed on an official source in this session.

## 9. Can an unregistered person be paid a referral fee?

- **Short answer: no, not safely.** Under section 2(zm), a person who "introduces, through any medium, prospective buyers" and receives "remuneration or fees or any other charges ... whether as commission or otherwise" is a real estate agent. Section 9 bars such a person from facilitating sales in a registered project without registration. A referral fee to an unregistered wealth manager therefore exposes the referrer to section 62 penalties. VERIFIED-SECONDARY for the statutory text; application to this fact pattern is my reading.
- **Promoter-side exposure (Arete paying an unregistered referrer):** no GujRERA circular found that expressly bars promoters from paying unregistered agents. NOT FOUND. Some state authorities (e.g., MahaRERA) have issued such directions; whether GujRERA has is unverified. Assume it is a compliance risk for Arete too.
- **Workable structures for the wealth manager programme (to be vetted by counsel):**
  1. Each wealth manager registers with GujRERA as an individual agent (Rs 10,000, 5 years) before any fee is paid. Mask360 can help them file.
  2. Wealth managers who will not register get no fee. Non-cash perks also risk counting as "any other charges".
  3. Do not route fees to unregistered people through Mask360's registration. Registration is personal to the applicant; a sub-agent who introduces buyers for remuneration is itself an agent. UNVERIFIED but follows from the definition.
  4. Paper every referral: written agreement, invoice from a registered agent, GST, TDS (section 194H on commission), and books per rule 14.

## 10. SEBI: registered investment advisers and referral fees

- **2020 amendment:** SEBI (Investment Advisers) (Amendment) Regulations, 2020, notification No. SEBI/LAD-NRO/GN/2020/22 dated 2020-07-03, effective 2020-10-01. VERIFIED-SECONDARY (taxguru reproduction).
- **Regulation 22A(1):** an investment adviser may provide implementation services to advisory clients in the securities market, provided that **"no consideration including any commission or referral fees, whether embedded or indirect or otherwise, by whatever name called is received"**, at the investment adviser's group or family level, for that service. VERIFIED-SECONDARY (quoted in taxguru and Vinod Kothari commentary).
- **Client-level segregation (2020):** a client is either an advisory client (no distribution consideration received by the IA or its group) or a distribution client (no advisory fee charged). Individual IAs cannot provide distribution services at all. VERIFIED-SECONDARY (Vinod Kothari, AZB commentary).
- **Regulation 15:** commentary cites regulation 15(2) together with 22A as the basis for the "no consideration from anyone other than the client" principle. Exact current wording NOT verified on sebi.gov.in.
- **Scope caveat:** these provisions are framed around securities and SEBI-regulated products. SEBI's 2024 Second Amendment Regulations (published 2024-12-16) and a January 2025 circular require IAs that advise on products outside SEBI's purview (SEBI itself has named real estate and gold) to disclose that such products are unregulated by SEBI and obtain client declarations. SEBI has also consulted on pushing such advice into a separate entity. So whether an RIA taking a real estate referral fee from a developer breaches the IA Regulations directly is NOT settled in sources reached. The conservative position: do not recruit RIAs for paid referrals.
- **Recruit instead:** wealth professionals who are not RIAs (for example mutual fund distributors with AMFI ARN, insurance POSPs, family office staff; practising CAs should first check ICAI rules on commissions), and require GujRERA registration before any fee. Bank relationship managers are bound by their bank's third-party product rules; exclude unless the bank signs.
- **Sources:** https://taxguru.in/sebi/sebi-investment-advisers-amendment-regulations-2020.html , https://vinodkothari.com/2020/07/partitioning-of-advisory-services-from-distribution-activities/ , https://www.azbpartners.com/bank/amendments-to-sebi-investment-advisers-regulations-2013/ , https://corporate.cyrilamarchandblogs.com/2024/08/revamping-the-investment-advisers-and-research-analysts-frameworks-the-sebi-way/ , https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/1358779330956.pdf (original 2013 regulations)
