## 5. Weather and timing, December to February

- **Weather:** dry season. Rain ~0 to 1 mm in January (climate sites). Daily highs ~29 to 31 C, lows ~14 to 21 C (sources vary). Midday sun is strong: shaded seating, water, caps. Mornings can be hazy on NH-48. Sources: https://weather-and-climate.com/vapi-gujarat-in-December-averages , https://en.climate-data.org/asia/india/gujarat/vapi-24372/ , https://championtraveler.com/dates/best-time-to-visit-vapi-in
- **Why this window matters for the relaunch:** the site is at its driest and the Kolak river at its lowest. Visitors will ask "where did the water reach in July 2026". Have a marked flood-level post, the mitigation works (drainage, plinth raise, embankment) visible, and the insurance offer in writing.
- **Day plan, car option (Mumbai):** depart 07:00 to 07:30, breakfast stop en route, arrive 11:00 to 11:30. Surat families depart 09:00, arrive ~11:00. Welcome 11:30, site walk 11:45 to 13:00, lunch 13:00 to 14:15, one-to-one sales desks 14:15 to 15:30. Mumbai cars leave by 15:30 to 16:00 to reach home by 20:00 to 20:30 (avoid night driving).
- **Day plan, train option (Mumbai):** 20901 dep 06:00, Vapi 07:53, shuttle, breakfast on site 08:30, same programme, return on 20902 (~18:15) or 12010 (~19:20).
- **Dates to avoid (check before fixing):** Christmas weekend 2026-12-26/27 and New Year 2027-01-01 to 01-03 (heavy Daman tourist traffic on NH-48 and Vapi hotels full); Uttarayan 2027-01-14 (Thursday) and the following weekend in Gujarat, as Surat families will be at kite festivities; Tata Mumbai Marathon is usually the third Sunday of January (2027-01-17 by that pattern, NOT verified) with morning road closures in Mumbai. Dec to Feb is also Gujarati wedding season: book hotels and caterers early.
- **Suggested six weekends (proposal only, all are Saturday/Sunday pairs):** 2026-12-05/06, 2026-12-12/13, 2027-01-09/10, 2027-01-23/24, 2027-02-06/07, 2027-02-20/21. Split each weekend: Mumbai families Saturday, Surat families Sunday, so lunch numbers stay ~40 to 50 per day and sales staff can give each family time.
