# Arete Group: Riverscape sales mandate

Everything Mask360 needs to start selling Riverscape villas on day 1 after Arete signs. Internal only. Nothing here goes to Arete or any third party without Kaavish's approval.

- **Client:** Arete Group. **Project:** Riverscape, Tukwada, Vapi. 195 villas, 4 and 5 BHK, Rs 2.5 to 4 Cr.
- **Mandate:** 90 days. Relaunch 20 villas, sell in blocks to companies and investors, close at six Open Weekends.
- **Commercials pitched:** Rs 12,00,000 fixed (50/25/25) plus 1% of agreement value per villa sold. Proposal sent as `M360-Riverscape-Commercial-Proposal.pdf` (2026-11-03).
- **Status (2026-11-03):** PITCHED. Waiting for Arete to sign and to share the break-even number.

## Day 1 in one line

Kaavish gets the break-even number and the promoter's 20 names from Arete. The corporate manager starts calling from `research/corporate-targets.csv`. The adviser manager starts calling from `research/adviser-targets.csv`. The sales director books vendors for Open Weekend 1 on 28 and 29 November.
