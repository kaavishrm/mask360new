# Team and hiring

We do not have most of these people today. Line them up before Arete signs so contracts start on day 1. Costs are estimates, monthly, INR.

## Structure

| Role | Type | Fixed / month | Success share | Based |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Engagement lead | Kaavish | n/a | n/a | Mumbai |
| Sales director | 3 month contract | Rs 1,00,000 | Yes | Surat or Vapi |
| Corporate manager | 3 month contract | Rs 60,000 | Yes | Vapi |
| Adviser manager | 3 month contract | Rs 60,000 | Yes | Mumbai |
| Desk executive x2 | 3 month contract or hire | Rs 35,000 each | Small bonus per booking | Vapi or remote |
| Creative lead, designer, video editor | Existing Mask360 team | Internal | No | Mumbai |

**Success share:** 30% of every Mask360 success fee goes to the sales team pool: sales director 15%, corporate manager 6%, adviser manager 6%, desk executives 3% split. Paid when Arete pays us. Example: one Rs 3.25 Cr villa gives Mask360 Rs 3,25,000; the pool gets Rs 97,500.

## Job descriptions

### Sales director (3 month contract)
**The job:** Run the sales engine day to day and close villas with Arete's sales head at six Open Weekends near Vapi.
**You will:**
- Run every Open Weekend on site: invitations, cars, lunch, the walk-through, the booking desk.
- Lead the daily standup and the Monday report.
- Coach the desk and the two managers on follow-up and conversion.
**You have:**
- 5+ years selling luxury residential (villas, bungalows, Rs 2 Cr plus) in Gujarat or Mumbai.
- Gujarati and Hindi, fluent. English for reports.
- Closed bookings at site events before. Comfortable asking for the cheque.
**Pay:** Rs 1,00,000 a month plus a 15% share of the success fee pool.
**Where to find:** ex-sales heads from Surat and Ahmedabad developers, luxury CP firms, LinkedIn search "sales head villa Surat", referrals from Arete's own CPs (only if not conflicted).

### Corporate manager (3 month contract)
**The job:** Build the list of the 60 largest employers in Vapi, Daman and Silvassa, and book 12 meetings with their HR heads, CFOs and promoters.
**You will:**
- Make 25 calls a day, mostly to executive assistants, in Gujarati.
- Run the 10 day outreach cadence per company and log everything in the tracker.
- Prepare each meeting brief and follow up on term sheets.
**You have:**
- 3+ years in B2B sales, industrial sales, or an industry association in South Gujarat.
- Contacts in GIDC Vapi, Sarigam, Daman or Silvassa are a big plus.
- Gujarati fluent. Polite persistence on the phone.
**Pay:** Rs 60,000 a month plus a 6% share of the success fee pool.
**Where to find:** ex-industrial sales reps (chemicals, packaging, utilities), association secretariat staff, Naukri and LinkedIn in Vapi and Valsad.

### Adviser manager (3 month contract)
**The job:** Sign 25 independent wealth managers, mutual fund distributors and CAs in Mumbai and Surat who introduce clients to Riverscape.
**You will:**
- Call 150 advisers, meet 60, sign 25.
- Brief each adviser on the investor lot, run the adviser site day, and make sure their introductions are registered.
- Handle adviser payouts with finance.
**You have:**
- 3+ years in wealth distribution, mutual fund distribution or private banking sales.
- A network among Gujarati advisers in Mumbai or Surat.
- Knows why SEBI-registered advisers cannot take referral fees.
**Pay:** Rs 60,000 a month plus a 6% share of the success fee pool.
**Where to find:** ex-AMC sales (distributor relationship managers), wealth firm business development staff, LinkedIn.

### Desk executive (2 roles)
**The job:** Run the follow-up desk on Arete's WhatsApp Business number and keep the tracker clean.
**You will:**
- Confirm every Open Weekend family, assign cars and send directions.
- Message every visitor within 24 hours and follow the day 3, 7 and 14 cadence.
- Update the tracker the same day, every day.
**You have:**
- 1 to 3 years in customer service, real estate pre-sales or event coordination.
- Gujarati and Hindi, fluent. Neat with spreadsheets.
**Pay:** Rs 35,000 a month plus Rs 2,500 per booking they followed up.
