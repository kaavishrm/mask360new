01 . The method

Three moves to sell the first 20.

Arete has run ads, email and channel partners. More of the same will not move a project after the July flood. We change the offer, sell villas in blocks, and close every sale at the site.

Move 1 . Weeks 1 to 2

Change the offer

  • Relaunch 20 villas only, with the post-flood redesign as the news.
  • A launch price for those 20, valid for 45 days, then it rises.
  • Flood cover in the agreement: Arete pays each villa's home insurance, flood included, for 5 years, and repairs flood damage to common areas.
  • A 10:90 plan: 10% at booking, 90% at possession.

The buyer's fear and the first cheque both shrink, and the deadline gives a reason to act now.

Move 2 . Weeks 3 to 12

Sell in blocks

  • Corporate deals: 3 to 10 villas per company as director residences and guest houses, pitched to the 20 largest employers in Vapi, Daman and Silvassa.
  • Investor lots of 5 villas through wealth managers, with a corporate lease signed now that starts at possession.
  • Advisers earn 3 to 4%, above channel partner rates, paid through a GujRERA-registered entity.

One signed company or investor is several villas. The fastest route back to break-even.

Move 3 . Weeks 3 to 12

Close at the site

  • Six Riverscape Open Weekends: cars from Mumbai and Surat, lunch at the experience centre, the engineer walking the new levels.
  • The launch price is honoured only for bookings made that weekend, cheque on the spot.
  • Every adviser and corporate contact brings families to one weekend.

At ₹2.5 to 4 Cr, the sale happens at the site, with the family there and a deadline in front of them.
