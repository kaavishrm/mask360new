01 . The idea

Everything runs on one promise.

When you buy a BMW, the service package is part of what you are buying: someone has already thought about what could go wrong, and covered it.

Riverscape does the same with the Flood Cover pack. Buyers already know about July. Instead of hoping they don't ask, we lead with the answer.

The pack is the theme of the film, the key visuals, the brochure and every conversation. It raises trust in Arete as a developer that stands behind its project. It is included for the first 20 relaunch villas, and Arete can offer it as a paid add-on after that.

What the Flood Cover pack holds
  • The factsThe July water level at the site, and the new finished levels of villas, promenade and parking, on one drawing.
  • The accessThe new ramp beside the gate: a second road for emergencies and parking.
  • The signatureA letter from an independent engineer on the redesign.
  • The coverHome insurance with flood cover for 5 years, paid by Arete, and a repair commitment for common areas.
  • The termsA 45-day launch price for 20 villas, and 10% at booking, 90% at possession.

Arete decides the cover and the price. We design the pack and build the campaign around it. We never call the site flood-proof: we show the facts.

Month 1: the relaunch.

23 November to 20 December. Nothing waits on the site: the campaign is built from the architect's renders and walkthroughs, the way we are building the Nandi Hills launch in Bengaluru before groundbreaking.

  • Week 123 to 29 Nov

    • Kickoff with Arete's marketing and sales teams. Arete shares renders, walkthroughs, drawings, the price sheet, past sales and lead data.

05 . The channels

How we reach the buyer.

Arete's earlier launch reached people. These channels reach them through someone they already trust.

Channel 1

Corporates, through HR heads and CXOs

We list the large companies and MNCs in Gujarat and Mumbai, starting with 20 and growing to 40. The HR head is the way in: they look after senior staff, relocations and guest houses.

What they get: the dossier and a Riverscape offer for their senior people, with a structured, written incentive for every booking that comes through them.

Channel 2

Wealth managers

Private wealth teams and independent advisers who look after Gujarati business families. Their clients ask them before buying.

What they get: a custom investor lot note per client, a referral agreement, and a site day for their clients. Paid only when a villa books.

Channel 3

HNI evening hosts

Hosts in Mumbai who run curated evenings for high net worth guests, such as wine and whisky tastings, 16 to 18 a year with around 50 guests each.

What we do: place Riverscape at these evenings, in front of exactly the buyer Arete wants, in a setting where they listen.

Channel 4

Channel partners

Arete's existing partners keep selling, now with a new offer and new material. We add a wider group of partners in Gujarat and Mumbai.

What they get: the Flood Cover pack, the film and the dossiers, in their clients' language.
