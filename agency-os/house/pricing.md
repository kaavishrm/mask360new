## Margin rules
- Minimum gross margin on any project: 40%. Below that, reprice or rescope.
- Retainer and subscription target margin: 50 to 60%.
- Vendor markup: India 20 to 30%, UAE 15 to 25%. Quote a production fee, never line-item vendor costs.
- Experiential: agency fee plus marked-up production, 50% advance, balance on milestones. Never fund production from our cash.

## How to build a quote
1. Internal hours times team rate (Rs 2,500 an hour blended DEFAULT; AED 300 DEFAULT).
2. Vendor costs times 1.25.
3. Plus 15% contingency.
4. Price so margin is at least 40% of the total.
5. Round to a clean number. Never quote 1,73,429.
6. Anchor: present the highest option first, about 25% above target.

## Proposal shape
Always three options. Option A (recommended, anchor), Option B (target), Option C (lean, at or just above floor, with scope removed). Scope moves, the price per unit of work does not.

## Negotiation rules
- Drop scope, never the rate.
- Every concession costs them something: longer term, faster payment, case-study rights, a referral.
- "Competitor quoted half": never ask who, never match. Offer specificity about what our number covers.
- "Trial month cheaper": offer a 90-day pilot at full rate with agreed KPIs.
- "Pay more when results come": only on the Systems performance bonus.
- Walk-away script when below floor: "I want to work with [Brand], the fit is right. But I'd be doing you a disservice by taking this on at a budget that doesn't allow us to do it properly. Let's revisit when the budget is closer to what the brief deserves. Meanwhile, happy to suggest what's achievable at your number with a different scope."

## Deal filter (run before any yes)
Margin above 40%. Brand aligned with the luxury and premium thesis. Payment terms acceptable. Executable with current team. Good reference. Likely to grow. Four or more noes: walk away, politely, door open.
