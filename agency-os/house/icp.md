## Brand tier points
Luxury 3, Premium 2, Mass 0.

## Timing points (0 to 3)
Live pitch or review: 3. Leadership change, incumbent turmoil, open mandate: 2 each, cap 3. Weaker signals (launch, funding, expansion, long tenure, renewal due): 1. Moved agency in the last 6 months: 0.

## Score and tiers
Score = fit + brand tier + timing, out of 10.
- **Prime** (score 9 to 10, or any live pitch on a Luxury or Premium brand): founder-led. Named email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, rooms. Never in a mass sequence.
- **Premium** (score 7 to 8): signal-triggered named outreach. LinkedIn plus named email. Apollo named sequence allowed.
- **Volume** (score 6 and under): Apollo category sequences, list mode.

The scorer re-scores an account whenever a signal lands. Score changes move tiers automatically.

## Who to reach at an account (in this order)
1. Founder, MD, CEO (founder-led brands and developers)
2. CMO, CBO, Chief Marketing and Digital Officer
3. Head of brand, marketing director, head of marketing communications
4. Brand manager on the specific brand (only after 1 to 3 are tried, and the message names the senior)
5. Procurement only when a tender or empanelment asks for it

Hotels: the property GM and director of sales and marketing, plus the cluster or brand marketing head.
Developers: founder or MD, then CMO, then the project head of a named launch.

## Markets
India (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Goa, Jaipur), UAE (Dubai, Abu Dhabi), Saudi (Riyadh, Jeddah, private sector only until a Saudi entity exists).

## Never contact (suppression)
- Anyone marked do_not_contact or who unsubscribed, on any channel.
- Current clients and their parent group's other brands, except through the account owner (growth agent).
- Competitor agencies' staff, except as partners or hires.
- Anyone already touched in the last 30 days on another channel, unless replying to them.
- People who asked not to be contacted, people below manager level with no named senior, students, job seekers with no fit.
- Brands in conflict with a current client in the same normalised category and the same city (os.accounts.city of both accounts; null means unknown). Prospecting agents (scorer, finder) keep an unknown-city match as a flagged possible conflict, never suppress on market; pitch, proposal and onboarding agents escalate amber before committing on a possible conflict.
