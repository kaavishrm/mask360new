# Commercial and legal terms

The contracts agent drafts from these. Templates in `../templates/` carry them. Have a lawyer review the templates once; after that the OS reuses them.

## Payment
- Subscriptions and retainers: invoiced on the 1st of each month, in advance, due in 15 days.
- Projects and diagnostics: 50% on signature, 50% on delivery. Projects over 60 days: 50% / 25% / 25% on milestones.
- Experiential: 50% of total on confirmation, production costs funded by the client before vendor commitments, balance within 15 days of the event.
- Media and third-party pass-through: client pays in advance, or the platform bills the client directly.
- Late payment: interest at 1.5% a month on overdue amounts. While Mask Management is a Udyam-registered micro or small enterprise, MSMED Act section 15 caps buyer terms at 45 days and section 16 adds interest at three times the RBI bank rate. This protection ends above Rs 100 Cr turnover.
- Work pauses when any invoice is 30 days overdue, after one written notice.

## Tax
- India: GST at 18% on services (SAC 998361 for advertising services, 998397 for event services; confirm with the CA). Clients deduct TDS, usually 2% under section 194C for advertising contracts or 10% under 194J for professional services; invoices show the expected TDS so collections reconcile.
- UAE: VAT at 5% where the UAE entity invoices. Export of services from India to UAE clients is zero-rated under GST with a LUT on file.

## Term and exit
- Subscriptions: 12-month term, auto-renews for 12 months unless either side gives 60 days' notice before renewal.
- Retainers: 6-month minimum, then 60 days' notice.
- Kill fee on projects cancelled after work starts: 50% of the remaining fee plus committed costs.

## Scope
- Scope is the deliverables table in the SOW. Two rounds of revision per deliverable are included. A third round or new items go on an addendum before work starts.
- Client approvals within 3 working days; silence after 5 working days counts as approval for scheduling purposes.

## Rights
- IP in final deliverables transfers to the client on full payment. Mask360 keeps rights to working files, tools, templates and methods, and may show the work in its portfolio unless the SOW says otherwise.
- Talent, music and stock licences are as stated in the SOW; usage beyond that is re-licensed at cost plus markup.
- AI-generated assets follow ASCI synthetic-content labelling.

## Other
- Mutual confidentiality for 3 years. Non-solicitation of each other's staff for 12 months.
- Liability capped at fees paid in the previous 3 months.
- Governing law and courts: Mumbai for the India entity, Dubai for the UAE entity.
