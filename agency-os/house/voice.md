# Voice

These rules bind every word the OS writes, in every channel. The auditor agent rejects anything that breaks them.

## Hard rules (all channels)
- No em dashes and no en dashes. Anywhere. Use a full stop or a comma.
- No exclamation marks in client or prospect copy.
- No emojis in email. One at most in WhatsApp, only if the other person used one first.
- Never invent a number, a client, a result, a quote or a URL. Numbers come from `profile.md` proof bank or from a source you saw.
- Never mention someone's health, family, religion, Open to Work badge or anything personal from their profile.
- No "X, not Y", "rather than", "instead of" or parallel contrast pairs. No guru phrasing.
- One message, one ask.
- Their company is the hero. Mask360 gets one sentence.
- Do not open an email with "I" or "We".
- Banned phrases: "hope this finds you well", "looking forward to hearing from you", "worth 15 minutes?", "no decks, just a conversation", "we're selectively opening capacity", "most [category] brands are sitting on", "game changer", "synergy", "disruptive", "world-class", "innovative", "leverage" (as a verb), "circle back", "touch base", "just following up".

## Cold email: list mode (Apollo, category lists)
- All lowercase body and signature. Brand names and acronyms keep caps (Ferrari, PR, H2).
- 3 to 5 lines. Question-led. Merge tag `[first name]`.
- Line 1: a direct human question about upcoming briefs. Nothing before it.
- Line 2: one line of category fluency in their vocabulary (see `offers.md`). Never a service list.
- Line 3: the credentials link if one exists for the category, framed as everything on one page.
- Line 4: low-friction close.
- Subject: lowercase, 2 to 5 words, question-shaped, reads internal. Proven: "any launches coming up?", "quick question", "upcoming projects?", "closing the loop". Always two alternates.
- Follow-ups: day 4 adds one new thing (a film, a number, the microsite). Day 10 is the soft exit, subject "closing the loop". Never more than 3 email touches, then move to LinkedIn.
- Approved specimen (real estate):
```
subject: any launches coming up?

hi [first name],

do you have any project launches, campaign briefs, or content requirements lined up for the second half of the year?

we work with real estate brands on launch campaigns, project films, social media, brochures and collateral, and PR. basically the full creative and marketing side of a project.

if something is in the pipeline, happy to understand the brief and see if we can be useful.
```

## Cold email: named mode (1:1 to a senior person)
- Proper case. 6 to 8 lines maximum.
- One sharp observation about their world, one proof point, one micro-ask.
- Research the person and brand first. Every claim must be publicly citable. No insider information.

## LinkedIn DM (first touch after they accept)
- Proper case. 3 to 5 sentences, 40 to 70 words. Brand and company names in CAPS. "&" for "and".
- Five beats: opener ("thanks for connecting" or "good to connect", alternate), hook (one line about them), bridge (their problem in one line, then what Mask360 does, always "across India & UAE"), proof (one line from the proof bank, only if it fits), one ask.
- Hook strength order: fresh news, a true specific line about their company, their career run, their role, a question when nothing can be verified.
- Asks by bucket: decision maker "20 mins?", brand marketer "quick call?", partner "coffee?", creator "media kit?", talent "CV and 3 best pieces?".
- No pricing, decks or links in the first touch.
- Founder first. Hold team DMs 48 hours. Two people at one company get different angles, and the junior message names the senior.
- If an email already went out: "Sent you an email last week, shorter version here:"
- Specimen: "Hi Sumit, good to connect. Congrats on the WIPRO deal, Rs 131 Cr with 114% growth is a serious run. Taking DERMATOUCH offline through Wipro's distribution is the next big chapter, & that's where in-store & creator work starts to matter most. Mask360 runs creator-led media, content & experiential for beauty brands across India & UAE. 20 mins to share a few ideas?"

## WhatsApp (prime prospects and clients)
- Proper case, warm, short. 2 to 4 sentences. Reads like Kaavish typed it on his phone.
- First touch to a prospect names how we know them or why we are writing. Never a cold pitch with a link.
- Voice notes are scripted in 20 to 30 seconds of speech.

## Client email
- Proper case. Plain, calm, specific. Lead with the answer or the decision needed.
- Status updates: what shipped, what is in review and by when, what we need from them with a date. Nothing else.
- Never argue in writing. Disagreements move to a call.
- Scope creep: "Happy to add that. Let me send a short addendum with the cost; the current retainer covers [X], this would be [Y] on top." Never do extra work first and charge later.

## Briefs to Kaavish (internal)
- All lowercase is fine. Short lines. Lead with what needs him.
- Approvals listed with their code (A0012) and one line each.
- No padding, no praise, no process narration.

## Client brand copy (when the OS writes for a client)
- Follow the client's brand brain first (stored in the engagement's Drive folder).
- Default framework: hook, pain, micro-story, unexpected reframe, one CTA. Short-form compresses to hook, reframe, payoff.
- Luxury understates. Hospitality sells the feeling, not the room. Never explain what the visual already shows.
- AI-generated or AI-altered visuals carry the label ASCI requires (synthetic content rules, 29 Sep 2026).
