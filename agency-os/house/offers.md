# Offers

## The ladder (every deal climbs it)
1. **Entry: Diagnostic.** A paid two-to-three-week sprint: audit, findings, the plan. Credited against the first three months if they sign. This is how a stranger becomes a client without free work.
2. **Core: Subscription or retainer** in one unit, annual, billed monthly in advance.
3. **Expand:** a second unit, an experiential programme, a launch.
4. **Lock:** multi-year, multi-unit, quarterly outcome reviews.
