# Master Services Agreement

Between **Mask Management**, trading as Mask360, {{registered address}} ("Mask360"), and **{{Client legal name}}**, {{address}} ("Client"). Effective {{date}}.

Have a lawyer review this template once before first use.

1. **Services.** Mask360 provides the services in each Statement of Work (SOW). Each SOW is governed by this agreement. If they conflict, the SOW wins for that engagement.
2. **Fees and payment.** As set in each SOW. Invoices are due within the SOW's terms, by default 15 days. Overdue amounts carry interest at 1.5% a month. Where Mask360 is a micro or small enterprise under the MSMED Act, sections 15 and 16 apply. Mask360 may suspend work on 7 days' written notice when any invoice is 30 days overdue.
3. **Taxes.** Fees exclude GST or VAT, which are added at the applicable rate. Client may deduct TDS as required by law and will issue certificates within the statutory time.
4. **Approvals and changes.** Client approves within 3 working days. Changes to scope are agreed in writing as an addendum before work starts. Two revision rounds per deliverable are included unless the SOW says otherwise.
5. **Intellectual property.** On full payment, Client owns the final deliverables. Mask360 keeps its pre-existing materials, working files, tools, templates and methods. Third-party materials (talent, music, stock, fonts) are licensed as stated in the SOW. Mask360 may show the work in its portfolio unless the SOW says otherwise.
6. **AI-assisted work.** Mask360 may use AI tools within the Client's Brand Brain rules. AI-generated or AI-altered assets are labelled as advertising codes require. Client inputs are not used to train third-party models where the tool allows that choice.
7. **Confidentiality.** Each party keeps the other's confidential information confidential for 3 years after the agreement ends.
8. **Non-solicitation.** Neither party hires the other's staff who worked on the engagement for 12 months after it ends without consent.
9. **Warranties.** Mask360 performs with reasonable skill and care. Client warrants that materials and claims it supplies are lawful and substantiated.
10. **Liability.** Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. Each party's total liability is capped at the fees paid in the 3 months before the claim, except for non-payment, confidentiality breach and wilful misconduct.
11. **Term and termination.** This agreement runs until terminated. Either party may terminate a SOW for material breach not cured in 15 days. SOW notice periods apply otherwise. Fees for work done and committed costs remain payable.
12. **Law and courts.** Laws of India and courts of Mumbai for the India entity. Laws of the UAE and courts of Dubai for the UAE entity.
