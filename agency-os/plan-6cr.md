# 6 crore by 31 March 2027: the plan

Made on 12 Nov 2026 from Gmail and Drive invoices, priced proposals, Apollo and LinkedIn history, then attacked by two independent reviewers and corrected. Every number in the source run carries its source; this file keeps the parts the OS works from.

## Verdict

Against 6 crore billed by 31 Mar 2027, Mask360 has Rs 33.8 lakh in sight from Swisse, Atenx and Flow Realty (only Rs 16 lakh of it contractually committed), so 6 crore is not reachable: the realistic range is Rs 16 lakh to Rs 1.76 crore, and about Rs 63 lakh is likely. Hitting 6 would take about 55 new wins to cover the Rs 5.66 crore gap, most of them signed by early February and including 7 events at an unproven Rs 30 lakh each, which means about 78 meetings a week, against 7 clients billed in the whole of the last 12 months and one confirmed person on the team.
