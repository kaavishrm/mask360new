# Run 2026-11-12

Did: income tax calculator for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27), aimed at belated returns due by 31 Dec 2026. Same regime comparison as the current-year page, plus a late-filing block: Section 234F fee (₹1,000 up to ₹5 lakh total income, else ₹5,000; nil below the exemption limit), Section 234A interest at 1% per month or part from the day after the due date on tax still unpaid after TDS, TCS and advance tax, and a lock that forces the new regime when the filing date is past the due date, since the old regime can only be chosen in a return filed on time. To share the code, the slab engine now lives in tools/lib/income-tax.mjs and build.mjs inlines any `lib` functions a tool lists; the FY 2026-27 page was moved onto it with no change in output. Also fixed "₹-0" rendering on both tax pages and let table label cells wrap below 480px so three-column tables fit on a phone.
