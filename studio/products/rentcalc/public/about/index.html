About Tenant Calc

Tenant Calc is a small set of calculators for people renting or letting a home in the United States: tenants moving out, landlords with one or two units, property managers who want a quick check.

How the tools work

Every calculation happens in your browser. Nothing you type leaves your device. There are no accounts and no email gates.

Accuracy

Each state rule names the statute section it comes from and the date it was verified against the legislature's published text. The tools are a guide, not legal advice; local ordinances can add to or change state rules, and a court decides disputes. For anything you will rely on, confirm with a local attorney or your state's tenant agency.

Found a mistake?

Write to hello@tenantcalc.com with the tool, the state and what you expected. Corrections are made quickly and noted on the page.
