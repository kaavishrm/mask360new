Why these tools exist

Most answers to "how long does my landlord have to return my deposit" are blog posts that disagree with each other and rarely say when they were last checked. These pages do one thing each, show the statute section the answer rests on and the date it was verified against the legislature's text, and run entirely on your device.

State law changes and cities add their own rules. Each page says plainly which states it covers and what it does not.

Tenant Calc is a set of free calculators and documents for landlords and tenants in the United States. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to a server.
