Why these tools exist

Most Indian finance tools online are buried under pop-ups, demand a phone number, or stamp a watermark on the document you just made. These do none of that. Each tool does one job, shows the formula it used, and runs entirely on your device.

The calculators follow the rules in force for the financial year shown on each page. When a Budget changes a slab or a limit, the page is updated and the date at the bottom moves.

Hisaab Tools is a set of free calculators and document generators for India. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to a server.
