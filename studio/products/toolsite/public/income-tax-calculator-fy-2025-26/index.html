Who this page is for

FY 2025-26 ran from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026 and is assessed in AY 2026-27. The due date for most individuals was 31 July 2026. If you missed it, you can still file a belated return under Section 139(4) of the Income-tax Act, 1961 until 31 December 2026, or until the assessment is completed if that is earlier. After that the only route is an updated return with additional tax. This page computes the tax itself and then what being late adds, so the amount you pay before filing is right the first time.

The slabs for FY 2025-26

Budget 2025 rewrote the new regime for this year: nil up to ₹4,00,000, then 5% to ₹8,00,000, 10% to ₹12,00,000, 15% to ₹16,00,000, 20% to ₹20,00,000, 25% to ₹24,00,000 and 30% above that. The Section 87A rebate of up to ₹60,000 removes all tax when taxable income is ₹12,00,000 or less, and marginal relief applies just above it. The standard deduction is ₹75,000 for salary and pension. The old regime kept its slabs: nil up to ₹2,50,000 (₹3,00,000 for senior citizens, ₹5,00,000 above 80), 5% to ₹5,00,000, 20% to ₹10,00,000 and 30% beyond, with a ₹12,500 rebate up to ₹5,00,000 of taxable income and a ₹50,000 standard deduction. These are the same figures as FY 2026-27, so the two calculators share one engine; what differs is the year, the due dates and the late-filing arithmetic.

What being late costs

Fee u/s 234F = ₹5,000, or ₹1,000 if total income is ₹5,00,000 or less Interest u/s 234A = 1% × unpaid tax × months from the day after the due date to the filing date (part of a month counts as a full month)

The fee applies to anyone who was required to file and files after the due date. If your income is within the basic exemption limit and none of the other filing triggers apply (foreign assets, large bank deposits, high electricity or travel spending), you are not required to file and the fee does not apply, though you may still want to file for a refund or a loan application. The interest is simple, not compounded, and is charged only on tax still unpaid: tax deducted at source, tax collected at source and advance tax come off first. Filing on 30 November against a 31 July due date is four months late: August, September, October and November. Filing on 1 December makes it five.

What else to expect

A belated return goes in under the new regime. Section 115BAC lets a taxpayer without business income opt for the old regime only in a return filed by the due date under Section 139(1), so once the due date has passed the old regime is off the table, and the calculator locks it when your filing date is late. The old regime column stays visible so you can see what the delay cost you in deductions. Losses other than house property loss cannot be carried forward from a belated return. Interest under Sections 234B and 234C applies separately if advance tax was short, and is not included here.

Which Act

Returns for AY 2026-27 are filed under the Income-tax Act, 1961, so the section numbers on this page are the familiar ones. The Income-tax Act, 2025 applies from tax year 2026-27 onwards; use the FY 2026-27 calculator for the current year.

Capital gains at special rates, agricultural income, relief under Section 89, and penalty for under-reporting are outside this calculator. Figures are those in force on the date shown at the bottom of the page.

Questions people ask

What is the last date to file the return for FY 2025-26?

31 December 2026 for a belated return, or the date the assessment is completed if earlier. The original due date for individuals without audit was 31 July 2026.

Is the late fee ₹5,000 even if I have no tax to pay?

Yes, if your total income is above the basic exemption limit you must file, and a late filing attracts the fee regardless of tax due: ₹1,000 up to ₹5 lakh of total income, ₹5,000 above it. Below the exemption limit there is no obligation to file and no fee.

Can I choose the old regime in a belated return?

No. The option to use the old regime must be exercised in a return filed by the due date. A belated return is assessed under the new regime, and a revised return cannot switch to the old regime unless the original return was filed on time. Those with business income also needed Form 10-IEA by the due date.

Does interest run on the whole tax or only on what is unpaid?

Only on what remains after TDS, TCS and advance tax. If your employer deducted the full amount, the interest is nil and only the fee applies.

Why are the slabs the same as FY 2026-27?

Budget 2025 set these slabs for FY 2025-26 and Budget 2026 left them unchanged, so both years use the same rates. The late-filing rules and due dates are what differ.
