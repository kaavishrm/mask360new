What a valid rent receipt contains

The tenant's name, the landlord's name, the amount, the month it covers, the address of the property, the date, the mode of payment and the landlord's signature. The landlord's PAN is needed when the rent paid in a year is more than ₹1,00,000. For cash payments above ₹5,000 a revenue stamp is affixed and signed across. The generator leaves a box for it when you pick cash.

Why employers ask for receipts

To allow the HRA exemption under Section 10(13A), the employer needs proof that rent was paid. Most payroll teams collect receipts for the whole year in January or February. Many accept quarterly receipts; some want one for each month. Generate twelve at once here and submit whichever set is asked for.

Paying rent to parents or a spouse

Rent paid to a parent is allowed if the money is actually transferred and the parent declares it as rental income. Paying rent to a spouse is generally not accepted. Keep bank records in either case.

Landlord without a PAN

If annual rent exceeds ₹1,00,000 and the landlord has no PAN, ask for a signed declaration with name and address in the format your employer provides. The exemption can still be claimed.

Questions people ask

Do I need a rent agreement as well?

Receipts are usually enough for payroll. Some employers also ask for the agreement, and the tax department can ask for it later, so keep a copy.

Is a digital or printed receipt valid?

Yes. A printed receipt signed by the landlord, or a PDF with the landlord's signature, is accepted. The format does not matter; the details do.

Can I generate receipts for a past financial year?

Yes. Pick the first month and number of months. Receipts are typically dated the last day of the month they cover, which is the default here.
