Income Tax Calculator for FY 2026-27 (AY 2027-28)

Enter your salary and deductions once. The calculator works out tax under both regimes for FY 2026-27 and tells you which one leaves more in your hand.

Deductions and exemptions (old regime only)

How this calculator works

For FY 2026-27 (income earned between 1 April 2026 and 31 March 2027) the slabs announced in Budget 2025 continue to apply. The new regime is the default. You can still opt for the old regime if its deductions bring your tax lower, and this page shows both so you do not have to guess.

Under the new regime the Section 87A rebate wipes out tax entirely when taxable income is ₹12,00,000 or less. With the ₹75,000 standard deduction, a salaried person pays nothing up to a gross salary of ₹12,75,000. Just above that line, marginal relief makes sure the tax never exceeds the amount by which income crosses ₹12 lakh.

Old regime slabs

Nil up to ₹2,50,000 (₹3,00,000 for senior citizens and ₹5,00,000 for those 80 and above), then 5% up to ₹5,00,000, 20% up to ₹10,00,000 and 30% above that. The 87A rebate of up to ₹12,500 applies when taxable income is ₹5,00,000 or less. The standard deduction is ₹50,000.

Surcharge and cess

Surcharge applies on tax when taxable income crosses ₹50 lakh: 10% up to ₹1 crore, 15% up to ₹2 crore, 25% up to ₹5 crore, and 37% above ₹5 crore under the old regime (capped at 25% under the new regime). Marginal relief ensures the extra tax never exceeds the extra income. A 4% health and education cess is added on tax plus surcharge in both regimes.

Which regime should you pick?

The old regime only wins when your deductions are large: typically a full 80C, a sizeable HRA exemption, home loan interest and health insurance together. For most salaried people with deductions under about ₹3.75 lakh, the new regime is lower. Run both here with your real numbers rather than relying on a rule of thumb.

Capital gains taxed at special rates, agricultural income, and relief under Section 89 are outside this calculator. The slabs shown are those in force when the page was last updated and will be revised if a Budget changes them.

Questions people ask

Is the new tax regime the default for FY 2026-27?

Yes. The new regime applies unless you opt for the old one. Salaried taxpayers can switch every year when filing their return. Those with business income can opt out of the new regime only once.

What is the maximum salary with zero tax in the new regime?

A gross salary of ₹12,75,000. After the ₹75,000 standard deduction, taxable income is ₹12,00,000, and the Section 87A rebate cancels the tax on it.

Does the calculator include HRA for the new regime?

No. HRA exemption, 80C, 80D and most other deductions are available only in the old regime. The new regime allows the standard deduction and the employer NPS contribution under 80CCD(2).

Which financial year does this calculator use?

FY 2026-27, which is assessment year 2027-28. The slabs are the ones in force on the date shown at the bottom of the page.
