HRA Exemption Calculator (Section 10(13A))

Enter your monthly basic, DA, HRA received and rent paid. The exemption is the least of three amounts, all shown below.

The three-condition rule

Section 10(13A) exempts the least of:

  1. The HRA you actually received.
  2. Rent paid minus 10% of salary (basic plus DA).
  3. 50% of salary if you live in Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata or Chennai, otherwise 40%.

The rest of the HRA is added to your taxable salary. Note that only those four cities count as metro for this purpose; Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune and Gurugram are 40% cities despite their rents.

Example

Basic ₹50,000 a month, HRA ₹20,000, rent ₹18,000 in Mumbai. Annual figures: HRA ₹2,40,000; rent minus 10% of salary is ₹2,16,000 minus ₹60,000, which is ₹1,56,000; 50% of salary is ₹3,00,000. The exemption is ₹1,56,000 and ₹84,000 of HRA is taxable.

What you need to claim it

Rent receipts for the year, a rent agreement if your employer asks, and the landlord's PAN when annual rent exceeds ₹1,00,000. If the landlord has no PAN, a declaration in the prescribed form is accepted. Paying rent to a parent is allowed as long as the money actually changes hands and the parent declares it as income.

If your employer did not consider HRA

You can still claim the exemption when filing your return by reducing the taxable salary figure and keeping the receipts for any later query. Form 16 will show the higher figure, so expect the return to differ from it.

New regime

HRA exemption is not available under the new tax regime. If HRA is a large part of your salary and you pay substantial rent, run both regimes in the income tax calculator before choosing.

Questions people ask

Can I claim HRA if I own a house?

Yes, if you live in a rented house in a different city for work and pay rent there. You can claim HRA for the rented house and home loan deductions on the owned house in the same year.

Is Bengaluru a metro for HRA?

No. For HRA purposes only Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata and Chennai qualify for the 50% limit. All other cities use 40%.

What if I changed cities during the year?

Calculate month by month with the limit applicable to each city and the rent paid in each, then add up the monthly exemptions. The annual figures above assume one city for the whole year.
