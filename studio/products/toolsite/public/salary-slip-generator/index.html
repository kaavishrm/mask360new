What goes on a salary slip

A payslip records what was earned and what was deducted in a month. The earnings side normally shows basic pay, house rent allowance, dearness allowance where applicable, conveyance, special allowance and any bonus or arrears. The deductions side shows the employee's provident fund contribution, ESI if covered, professional tax levied by the state, and TDS on salary. The difference is net pay, which should match the bank credit.

Typical components explained

Basic is usually 40% to 50% of CTC and drives PF, gratuity and HRA limits. HRA is often 40% or 50% of basic. Provident fund is 12% of basic (and DA) from the employee, with a matching employer share that does not appear as a deduction. Professional tax is a state levy, at most ₹2,500 a year, deducted monthly in most states. TDS is the income tax the employer withholds based on your declared regime and investments.

Who uses a generated payslip

Small businesses and startups without payroll software, employers of household staff, and freelancers who need to document retainers for a loan or visa application. Banks and embassies look for the employer's name, the employee's details, a month, a breakdown and a net figure; this template includes all of them.

A note on accuracy

The generator does not compute PF, professional tax or TDS for you, because the right figures depend on state, wage ceilings and the employee's tax declaration. Enter the amounts from your payroll working. The income tax calculator on this site helps with the TDS figure.

Questions people ask

Does the payslip need a signature or stamp?

Not usually. Payslips generated by software carry a line saying no signature is required, which this template includes. Some banks ask for a stamped copy; print it and have the employer stamp it if so.

Should employer PF appear on the slip?

Employer contributions are part of CTC but are not deducted from gross pay, so they are typically shown separately or not at all. This slip shows only the employee share as a deduction.

Can I use this for a domestic worker or driver?

Yes. Fill in your own name as the employer, leave PF and ESI at zero unless you register, and keep a copy for both parties.
