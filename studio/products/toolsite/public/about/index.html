About Hisaab Tools

Hisaab Tools is a small collection of calculators and document generators for people who deal with money in India: salaried employees, freelancers, landlords, tenants, small business owners and the accountants who help them.

How the tools work

Every calculation happens in your browser. The page loads, and from then on nothing you type leaves your device. There are no accounts, no email gates and no watermarks on generated documents.

Accuracy

Each calculator states the financial year and the rules it applies. The formulas are shown on the page so you can check them. The tools are a guide, not advice. For anything you will sign or file, confirm the numbers with a chartered accountant.

Found a mistake?

Write to hello@hisaabtools.in with the tool name, the inputs you used and what you expected. Corrections are made quickly and noted on the page.
