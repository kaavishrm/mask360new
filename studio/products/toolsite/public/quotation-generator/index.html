What a good quotation contains

A quotation has no statutory format, but a customer, a bank or an auditor expects the same things on every one: who is quoting, to whom, a number and a date, a validity period, the items with quantity and rate, the tax shown separately, the total in words, and the terms under which the price holds. A quotation that leaves out validity or payment terms invites the two arguments that cost small businesses the most: a price held open for months and an unpaid advance.

Showing GST on a quote

Quote the price before tax and show GST as a separate line at the rate that will apply on the invoice. The customer sees the full outgo, and if a registered customer will claim the credit, they can see the taxable value at a glance. The generator splits the tax into CGST and SGST when your state matches the customer's, and shows IGST otherwise, the same way the eventual invoice will. If you are a composition dealer or unregistered, set every line to 0%.

A quotation is not an invoice

No tax is payable on a quotation, no input credit arises from it, and it does not need a GSTIN series or e-invoice registration. The footer says so, so that nobody books it as a purchase. Once the customer accepts, issue a tax invoice with the GST invoice generator using the same line items, or a bill of supply if you do not charge GST.

Validity and terms worth stating

Thirty days is the common validity for services; for goods with volatile input costs, seven to fifteen days is safer. State the advance you need to start, when the balance falls due, what is included and what costs extra, the delivery or completion period, and who bears freight and insurance for goods. For services, name the number of revision rounds. Each line you write here is one fewer email later.

Acceptance

The two signature blocks at the bottom let the customer countersign the quote. A signed quotation with clear terms is a contract in substance and is usually enough for a small engagement; larger ones still need a work order or agreement.

The generator does not number quotations automatically; keep your own series. Rules on tax rates are those in force on the date at the bottom of the page.

Questions people ask

Should I quote inclusive or exclusive of GST?

Exclusive, with GST on a separate line, unless you are selling to consumers who expect one all-in price. Business customers compare taxable values and claim credit on the tax.

Is a quotation legally binding?

A quotation is an offer. Once the customer accepts it within the validity period, a contract forms on those terms. That is why the validity date and the terms matter, and why the acceptance block is there.

What is the difference between a quotation, an estimate and a proforma invoice?

In practice the first two are the same document. A proforma invoice looks like an invoice but is issued before supply, often so a customer can arrange payment or an import permit. None of the three is a tax invoice.

Can I convert this into an invoice?

Not automatically. Re-enter the lines in the GST invoice generator once the order is confirmed. Keeping the two separate avoids accidentally sending a quotation numbered like an invoice.
