In-Hand Salary Calculator: CTC to monthly take-home for FY 2026-27

Enter the CTC from your offer letter and how it is structured. The calculator removes the employer costs that never reach you, then the deductions that come off your payslip, and shows the monthly amount you can actually spend.

Why CTC and in-hand salary are so different

Cost to company is everything the employer spends on you. In-hand salary is what reaches your bank account. The gap has two parts: amounts that are in your CTC but never paid out monthly, and amounts that are paid out but deducted before you see them.

Gross salary = CTC minus employer PF minus employer ESI minus gratuity provision minus variable pay In-hand = Gross minus employee PF minus professional tax minus ESI minus TDS

What comes out before gross

Employer PF. The employer contributes 12% of your PF wages (basic plus dearness allowance). From 17 September 2026 the statutory wage ceiling is ₹25,000 a month, up from ₹15,000, so an employer who contributes only on the ceiling pays at most ₹3,000 a month. Many employers contribute on full basic instead. Both the employer and employee shares go to your PF account, so the money is yours, just not now. Of the employer share, 8.33% of wages (up to ₹2,083 a month at the new ceiling) goes to the pension scheme.

Gratuity. Some offer letters add 4.81% of basic as a gratuity provision. It is paid only if you complete five years, and it is calculated on your last drawn basic, so the provision in your CTC is an estimate, not money set aside for you.

Variable pay. Performance bonus, retention bonus and joining bonus are part of CTC but are paid separately, often once a year and not in full. The calculator removes them from the monthly figure and adds them back to the annual total, taxed.

What comes off your payslip

Employee PF is 12% of PF wages, on the same base the employer uses. Professional tax is a state levy with a ceiling of ₹2,500 a year. Maharashtra charges ₹175 a month between ₹7,500 and ₹10,000, then ₹200 a month and ₹300 in February, and since April 2023 exempts women earning up to ₹25,000. Karnataka charges ₹200 a month and ₹300 in February from ₹25,000, the February figure since April 2025 under Karnataka Act 33 of 2025. West Bengal's new schedule from 1 October 2026 exempts salaries up to ₹20,000 and tops out at ₹208 a month. Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat (₹200 above ₹12,000) and Madhya Pradesh have their own slabs. Chennai and Kerala collect half-yearly on schedules revised from October 2024. Delhi, Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, Rajasthan, Punjab and Goa levy none, and Odisha abolished its tax from 1 April 2026. ESI applies at 0.75% of gross when gross is ₹21,000 a month or less and the establishment is covered. TDS is your estimated income tax for the year, deducted in equal monthly instalments by the employer.

New regime or old

Under the new regime the only deduction is the ₹75,000 standard deduction, and tax is nil when taxable income is ₹12 lakh or less, which for a salaried person means a gross salary of up to ₹12.75 lakh. Under the old regime you get a ₹50,000 standard deduction, the HRA exemption, professional tax and whatever you claim under 80C, 80D and the rest. Switch the regime above and the TDS line changes; the income tax calculator shows the full comparison.

Where this calculator simplifies

PF wages under the Code on Social Security include basic, dearness allowance and retaining allowance, and if other allowances exceed half of total pay the excess is treated as wages. The calculator uses basic alone. Employer NPS contributions, meal cards, leave travel allowance, company cars and other perquisites are not modelled; enter their value in the deductions field if your employer structures them. Professional tax slabs are the ones in force when the page was last updated and vary by municipality in Tamil Nadu and Kerala. Surcharge above ₹50 lakh and marginal relief are included.

Questions people ask

Is the PF deduction 12% of basic or 12% of ₹25,000?

It depends on your employer. The law requires contributions on wages up to the ₹25,000 ceiling that applies from 17 September 2026. Employers may contribute on full basic, and many do. Check your payslip: if your PF is ₹3,000 and your basic is higher than ₹25,000, your employer uses the ceiling.

Why is my in-hand lower than this calculator shows?

Usually one of three things: the employer deducts TDS unevenly, you have not submitted investment proofs so the old-regime deductions were not applied, or part of your CTC is a benefit such as insurance, food coupons or a notice-period buyout that the calculator does not know about. Does a higher basic mean a higher in-hand salary?

No, usually the opposite. A higher basic raises PF and gratuity, which lower the monthly figure, and raises HRA, which helps only if you pay rent and use the old regime. A lower basic with a larger special allowance gives more cash now and less retirement saving.

What changed with the EPF wage ceiling in September 2026?

The ceiling for mandatory PF, pension and insurance coverage rose from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 a month on 17 September 2026. Employees earning between ₹15,001 and ₹25,000 who were outside PF are now covered, and those contributing on the old ₹15,000 cap now contribute on actual wages up to ₹25,000. Take-home pay falls slightly and PF savings rise.

Which financial year does the tax calculation use?

FY 2026-27, called tax year 2026-27 under the Income-tax Act, 2025. The slabs are the ones in force on the date shown at the bottom of the page.
