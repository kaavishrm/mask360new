What a GST tax invoice must contain

Rule 46 of the CGST Rules lists the required fields: the supplier's name, address and GSTIN; a consecutive invoice number unique for the financial year; the date; the recipient's name, address and GSTIN if registered; HSN code for goods or SAC for services; description, quantity and unit; taxable value; the rate and amount of CGST, SGST or IGST; the place of supply with state name; whether tax is payable on reverse charge; and the signature of the supplier or an authorised person. This generator covers each of these.

CGST and SGST, or IGST?

Compare the supplier's state with the place of supply. Same state: charge CGST and SGST, each at half the rate. Different state: charge IGST at the full rate. The invoice above does this automatically from the two state fields. For services, the place of supply is normally the recipient's location when the recipient is registered.

HSN and SAC codes

Businesses with turnover up to ₹5 crore must show at least four digits of the HSN code on B2B invoices; above ₹5 crore, six digits on every invoice. Services use six-digit SAC codes starting with 99. Common ones: 998314 for IT design and development, 998311 for management consulting, 998361 for advertising, 996511 for road transport of goods.

Invoice numbering

Numbers must run consecutively within a financial year and may include letters, hyphens and slashes, up to 16 characters. A fresh series can start each April. Do not reuse or skip numbers; a cancelled invoice is still reported.

E-invoicing

If your aggregate turnover exceeded ₹5 crore in any year since 2017-18, B2B invoices must be registered on the Invoice Registration Portal and carry an IRN and QR code. This generator is for businesses below that threshold or for B2C invoices; it does not produce an IRN.

Saving and sending

Questions people ask

Can I issue a GST invoice without a GSTIN?

No. Only registered persons can charge GST. If you are unregistered, issue a bill of supply with no tax lines. Leave the GSTIN and set GST to 0% on each line to use this layout as a plain invoice.

What is the difference between a tax invoice and a bill of supply?

A tax invoice shows GST charged and lets a registered buyer claim input credit. A bill of supply is issued by composition dealers and for exempt supplies; it shows no tax.

Should I round the total?

Yes. Section 170 of the CGST Act allows rounding of the tax amount to the nearest rupee. The generator rounds the invoice total and shows the round-off line when it applies.

Is a digital signature required?

A physical or digital signature of the supplier or an authorised signatory is required. A printed invoice signed by hand is fine. A PDF can carry a digital signature or be signed after printing.
