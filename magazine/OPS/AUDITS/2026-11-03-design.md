# Design audit, 2026-11-03

Self-audit of the launch design against the brief (compete with GQ India, Condé Nast, The Established, The Nod; corporate goss for an industry that pays for premium). Verdict: the typography, grid and chrome are right; the colour and the generated imagery are wrong and cheap. Findings, worst first.

| # | Finding | Why it fails | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Six pure template colours (#FF3332, #FF00B7, #F9C728, #6E2CF4, #3FAD57, #0000EE) used as full-bleed grounds on covers, verdict slides, the footer, the promo band and the watchlist | The reference uses them only as 14px label text. At full bleed they read as a children's app or a Canva deck. Luxury, hospitality and agency buyers will not put their brand next to them | New palette: ink, warm paper, one tea accent, six muted section inks used only for labels, rules and the spine. Grounds are paper and ink, nothing else |
| 2 | Covers built from a giant word ("logo loop", "five tables") on a colour block | A trick that reads as a template after three slides, and it says nothing. The reference covers are photographs | Photo-led covers built from the story's own media receipt: the evidence image top, an ink panel with the headline below. Scorecards keep the number cover. Typographic covers only when no evidence exists |
| 3 | Monogram circles ("KR", "TV", "LP") on item slides and the watchlist | Placeholder avatars. They look like a CRM, not a magazine | Item slides carry a large index numeral and the evidence image where one exists. The watchlist is a typographic list with counts |
| 4 | Red reveal footer and red promo band with a fake barcode | Pastiche of the reference's demo content; the barcode is a prop | Ink footer and ink promo band, paper type, no props |
| 5 | Hero tile: a faded giant word under a dark gradient | Muddy, and it fights the overlaid headline | Hero is the cover photograph with a scrim; with no photograph, an ink tile with the headline alone |
| 6 | Tea slides: a white field with a pull line and a paragraph, 55 percent empty | Reads as unfinished | Paper ground, a tea-coloured rule, the type set larger and anchored, page furniture consistent |
| 7 | Verdict slides on pure colour with white serif | The one slide people screenshot was the loudest | Ink ground, paper serif, "The read" in the tea accent. Quiet and authoritative |
| 8 | Receipt slides: fine, but the kicker pill and the label colours around them were loud | See 1 | Labels in the section ink, pill in the tea accent |
| 9 | Page ground pure white | Correct for the reference, but with the new palette paper is warmer and reads more like a printed title | Paper ground site-wide, white only inside receipt frames |
| 10 | Masthead, nav, hairlines, grid, card anatomy, story page | Faithful to the reference and working | Keep |

Rule added to the house: colour is a label, never a ground. The photograph is the colour.
