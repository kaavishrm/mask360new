## How the pricing works

We build the audience first and price from the numbers. The rate card below shows the floors. Above the floor, the price of each placement is computed from the trailing 30 day reach of the format you are buying into, and the numbers behind the price are published here every month once the title is 90 days old. You are buying a proven position, not a promise.

## What we will not sell

A rating. A kicker. Silence on a story. An unlabelled post. The order of anything. More than two paid items in any Edit. More than two Brand Drops a day. Any slot to a brand in a Receipts story for 90 days.

## The audience

Agency people, brand marketers, CMOs, founders, event producers, restaurant and hotel operators, PR teams and creators in Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Goa and Dubai, and the culturally fluent 20 to 40 year olds who follow the brands they run. Audience numbers and the reach behind each price are published here monthly once the title is 90 days old.
