## The tea

Sunburn put its full Mumbai bill out on 21 October, per [Time Out Mumbai](https://www.timeout.com/mumbai/news/sunburn-2026s-going-big-beyond-4-headliners-check-out-50-performers-102126). The Chainsmokers and Jason Derulo take Day 1 on 18 December, DJ Snake and Sebastian Ingrosso take Day 2 on 19 December, MC Haits hosts, and below the four headliners the list runs past 50 names: Acraze, Emma Heesters, Laidback Luke, NERVO, Nucleya, Ookay and Sofi Tukker in the first tier, then a 40-plus block of Indian and regional DJs. Time Out calls it the first time the festival has carried this many headliners. [EDM House Network](https://edmhousenetwork.com/sunburn-festival-reveals-massive-two-day-lineup-for-dec-2026/) counts the same way: four headliners, seven named supporting acts, more than 40 more. We scrolled. It kept going.

The venue is Mahalaxmi Racecourse, the main event is two days rather than last year's three, and there is a closing party at NSCI Dome on 20 December, per [Sunburn's own announcement of 26 May](https://sunburn.in/?p=23421). Last year's Mumbai debut ran three days at Infinity Bay in Sewri and drew mixed reactions on setup, location and crowd management, per [Ticket Fairy](https://www.ticketfairy.com/word/2026/06/02/sunburn-continues-mumbai-era-with-mahalaxmi-racecourse-as-new-venue). This is the 19th edition. The [event listing](https://sunburn.in/event/sunburn-festival-2026/) names Spacebound as organiser.

The price: general access from INR 3,500 for a single day, sold exclusively on BookMyShow, per Time Out. [Esquire India](https://www.esquireindia.co.in/culture/books-and-music/sunburn-festival-2026-mumbai-dates-venue-tickets-lineup-and-everything-you-need-to-know) put the two-day pass near INR 7,000 in September. The categories, per [Time Out's DJ Snake piece of 9 September](https://www.timeout.com/india/news/dj-snake-returns-to-india-to-headline-sunburn-festival-2026-this-december-090926), run GA, VIP, Tuborg, Fanpit, The Core Lounge, VVIP Single Table and VVIP Platinum Table. The beer brand has its own ticket tier, which tells you exactly how the sponsor conversation went.

## The receipts

The credits. Sunburn's May post carries "presented by RuPay" and "driven by Hyundai", with Tuborg Zero packaged drinking water in the title line. Time Out's 21 October piece lists Tuborg, RuPay, Hyundai and Kinder Bueno, the last credited as "elevated by". That is a payments network, a carmaker, a beer brand's zero-alcohol water and a chocolate bar. Not our words, theirs.

The missing name. Arre, where is Croma? In May 2023 Croma and Sunburn announced a four-year title sponsorship under which all Sunburn events across the nation would be called Croma Sunburn, per [MediaNews4U](https://www.medianews4u.com/croma-becomes-title-partner-for-sunburn-music-festival/). Croma had been the official electronics partner for the 2022 edition before that, per [Mediainfoline](https://www.mediainfoline.com/alliances/croma-partners-with-the-biggest-music-festival-in-india-sunburn-2022-as-their-official-electronics-partner). Four years from 2023 runs through 2026. The 2026 Mumbai credits we have read, on sunburn.in and in Time Out, do not carry Croma. We found no release saying the deal ended and none saying it continues. Sponsorship terms change, and festivals rarely announce the ones that lapse. Croma and Sunburn can write to tea@mask360.agency. We will read it twice.

The scale. For the 2025 Mumbai debut, Sunburn reported 1,000-plus security personnel, 300-plus police officers and a 15,000 square foot LED screen, per [WION](https://www.wionews.com/entertainment/lifestyle/mumbai-gears-up-for-its-first-ever-sunburn-festival-with-safety-plans-1-000-security-personnel-300-mumbai-police-officials-1765974234318/amp). Its own 2025 survey, quoted in the May post, says close to half the crowd came from outside Mumbai. Karan Singh, Sunburn's CEO, told [Outlook India](https://www.outlookindia.com/art-entertainment/hollywood/the-chainsmokers-india-tour-2026-duo-to-headline-sunburn-festival-in-mumbai) in June the aim is to "create large-scale cultural moments where music, fandom, production and community come together".

## The read

Sunburn's Mumbai bill carries more headliners than it ever has, and the sponsor stack is just as crowded: RuPay presents, Hyundai drives, Tuborg sells a ticket tier, Kinder Bueno sits somewhere in the middle. The name we expected to see first, Croma, is the one we cannot find. If the title deal is still running, the festival has put its title partner behind four other logos. If it is not, year four of a four-year deal went quiet. Either way, the business of this festival is now told by the credits line, and the credits line has a chocolate bar on it. December's credits are the test.
