Moves, week 45. Seven on-the-record moves between 15 October and 3 November, sourced to the trade press or the company's own release.

## Kiran Ramamurthy, from Grey 82.5 to VML India

VML India named Kiran Ramamurthy chief executive on 28 October, effective 1 December, per [Campaign India](https://www.campaignindia.in/article/kiran-ramamurthy-to-succeed-babita-baruah-as-vml-india-ceo/4y8cd5s5sk77h08ns86a1ve9nt). He succeeds Babita Baruah, CEO since 2024, who moves to a senior regional role building the Ford business for VML. Ramamurthy is the CEO of Grey 82.5 and led the integration of Grey and 82.5 Communications inside The Ogilvy Group. Hephzibah Pathak, CEO of WPP Creative India: "VML enters this next chapter in a position of strength", per [afaqs](https://www.afaqs.com/people-spotting/vml-india-appoints-kiran-ramamurthy-as-ceo-12761362) and Campaign India.

## Shouvik Roy, back to Ogilvy Group as CEO of Grey 82.5

A day later, 29 October, The Ogilvy Group named Shouvik Roy CEO of Grey 82.5, also from 1 December, per [Campaign India](https://www.campaignindia.in/article/shouvik-roy-returns-to-ogilvy-group-as-ceo-of-grey-82-5/4876ezsfx9pv63v3h72ff8jedx) and [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/brand-makers/shouvik-roy-to-take-over-as-ceo-of-grey-82-5-ws-l-113641.htm). Roy was president and head of Ogilvy Delhi, co-founded the agency behind the Paperboat brand, and later helped build G.O.A.T Brand Labs. [Manifest Media](https://manifest-media.in/advertising/291026/shouvik-roy-moves-back-to-ogilvy.html) puts his last full-time Ogilvy stint at 2019 to 2022. VR Rajesh, CEO of The Ogilvy Group India: "In Shouvik Roy, Grey 82.5 gets a leader who perfectly embodies our challenger ethos." Ramamurthy and Roy run a handover through November.

## Neville Shah, from Kinnect CCO to next move not known

[Campaign India](https://www.campaignindia.in/article/neville-shah-moves-on-from-kinnect/42j7n9abrd5vg08qypawggtgxs) reported on 15 October that Neville Shah has stepped down as chief creative officer of Kinnect and is serving his notice period. He joined in May 2024 after ten years at Ogilvy, where he was last senior executive creative director. Kinnect has operated inside Omnicom Advertising India since the group's new structure took effect on 1 January, per [afaqs](https://www.afaqs.com/news/advertising/aditya-kanthy-and-prasoon-joshi-to-steer-omnicoms-india-reboot-10829934). Campaign India says his next move is not known. That is where the record stops.

## TBWA\Lintas wins Starbucks India

Tata Starbucks appointed TBWA\Lintas, part of Omnicom Advertising India, as creative agency of record on 2 November after a multi-agency pitch, per [Campaign India](https://www.campaignindia.in/article/starbucks-india-appoints-tbwa-lintas-as-its-creative-agency-of-record/4qssv4hqsxma0sr8bn68mvb3x2). The mandate covers brand campaigns, social media, digital activations and consumer engagement. The account arrives with 505 stores in 81 cities, per Tata Consumer's Q2 FY27 results reported by [Indian Retailer](https://www.indianretailer.com/news/tata-consumer-products-q2-results-show-strong-growth-india-business). Lintas is a name Omnicom kept alive on 1 January 2026 when it retired MullenLowe, per [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/advertising/ddb-fcb-mullenlowe-are-dead-somehow-lintas-lives-85199.htm). Ten months later it is winning pitches.

## Tanya Goyal, Everest Brand Solutions, to chair of AAAI's INDIE Council

The Advertising Agencies Association of India set up a council for independent agencies on 30 October and named Tanya Goyal of Everest Brand Solutions its chair, per [Campaign India](https://www.campaignindia.in/article/aaai-sets-up-indie-council-names-tanya-goyal-chair/4mwxvygf854w8wpbj6dxcqvsn1). The agenda is technology access, talent retention and regional representation. The first initiative is an AI boot camp after Diwali. AAAI vice-president Jaydeep Gandhi: "We will start with basics in prompt engineering." On 3 November AAAI also held the first meeting of its Media Council, chaired by Kartik Sharma, CEO-India of Omnicom Media, with WPP Media, Publicis Media, Havas and Madison in the room, per [Campaign India](https://www.campaignindia.in/article/media-agency-representatives-unite-under-new-aaai-council/4w32qxp00e92jy22paazefs4c6).

## Mohit Rathi, from Porter VP customer growth to Porter CMO

Porter promoted Mohit Rathi to chief marketing officer on 3 November, per [afaqs](https://www.afaqs.com/people-spotting/porter-appoints-elevates-mohit-rathi-to-chief-marketing-officer-12779093). He was vice president, customer growth and engagement, since 2023 and joined the logistics company more than a decade ago as an expansion manager, launching Hyderabad and Chennai. The same release named Gaurav Jain, over a decade at Amazon, as CFO and Shubham Gupta, who joined in 2015, as CTO. Uttam Digga, co-founder and CEO: "Shubham and Mohit have been pillars of this company, the core of how we work."

## Kanika Juneja, from Nestlé to Apollo Tyres

Kanika Juneja announced on LinkedIn that she has started as head of marketing, India and South East Asia, at Apollo Tyres, per [afaqs](https://www.afaqs.com/people-spotting/apollo-tyres-appoints-kanika-juneja-as-head-of-marketing-india-south-east-asia-12780001) on 3 November. She spent over six years at Nestlé, most recently as category marketing head for Maggi Noodles, and nearly six at GSK Consumer Healthcare on Horlicks. Noodles to tyres is a bigger jump than most CMOs make.

## The read

WPP India fills one CEO chair from inside the group and one with a returning alumnus. Omnicom's revived Lintas name wins a pitch. The trade body builds a lane for the independents. The holdco reshuffle has stopped talking and started hiring.
