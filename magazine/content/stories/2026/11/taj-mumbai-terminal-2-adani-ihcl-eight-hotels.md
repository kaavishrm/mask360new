## The tea

On 29 October, Adani Airport Holdings and the Indian Hotels Company signed what [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/india/aahl-ihcl-sign-one-of-indias-largest-multi-hotel-agreements-for-8-hotels-with-2000-rooms) calls one of India's largest multi-hotel operating agreements: eight hotels, more than 2,000 rooms, Adani building and IHCL operating. The brand split, [per IHCL's release on Hospitality Net](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/news/4135179/ihcl-and-adani-airports-sign-multi-hotel-agreement-for-eight-hotels-across-four-cities.html), is three Taj, two Vivanta, one Gateway and two Ginger. Luxury, upscale, mid-scale, one landlord. Suno, because the city split is where the goss is.

Mumbai gets one hotel and it is the trophy: a Taj under construction at Terminal 2 of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International. Ahmedabad gets a Taj and a Vivanta, both at advanced stages of development. Lucknow gets a Vivanta and a Ginger. Guwahati gets the most doors, three: a Taj, a Gateway and a Ginger. Opening dates? There are none for any of them in the release, [per The Moodie Davitt Report](https://moodiedavittreport.com/?p=486864), which also notes that Adani now has 15 hotel partnerships across its airport network.

Then the land, which is the actual story. Adani Airport City Limited, formed in June, holds a land bank of more than 655 acres across eight airports in seven states, nearly 440 acres of it in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai. Phase one is more than INR 20,000 crore to develop about 22 million sq ft across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Lucknow, Jaipur and Guwahati, [per News Vibes of India](https://newsvibesofindia.com/adani-airports-ltd-signs-pact-with-indian-hotels-ltd-to-develop-8-hotels/). The hotels are the amenity layer on a real estate play. Adani operates eight airports: Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Lucknow, Mangaluru, Jaipur, Guwahati, Thiruvananthapuram and Navi Mumbai International.

IHCL is not the only operator on Adani tarmac. In May, Adani signed IHG Hotels and Resorts for five hotels and 1,500 keys across Navi Mumbai, Jaipur, Mangaluru and Thiruvananthapuram, including the Indian debut of Kimpton, per Free Press Journal. So the IHCL deal takes Taj into Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Lucknow and Guwahati and leaves Navi Mumbai, for now, to IHG. Remember that sentence in six months.

## The read

The airport is becoming the high street, and Taj just took the anchor store. Two things we think the numbers say. First, the brand mix is a tell: three Taj for eight sites is a luxury operator agreeing to run Ginger and Gateway rooms because the landlord controls 440 acres of Mumbai. Second, the number that is missing matters more than the ones announced. No opening dates, no room rates, no per-hotel investment. A multi-hotel operating agreement is a letter of intent with a press photo. Watch Navi Mumbai: IHG has it, IHCL does not, and that is the airport the whole city is about to route through. If Taj wants the full set, the next release is already being drafted, and we will read that one twice too.
