Lakmé Fashion Week x FDCI ran at Taj Palace, New Delhi, from 13 to 18 October: six days, over 35 shows and over 30 stalls in the buyers' lounge, per [t2's highlights](https://t2online.in/goodlife/fashion-beauty/lfw-x-fdci-2026-highlights--t2os-picks-shows-that-defined-the-landscape-of-modern-indian-fashion/2008838). Lakmé and Tira presented péro's Spring Summer 2027 as the opening show, per [the release](https://easternherald.com/2026/10/09/lakme-and-tira-come-together-to-present-pero-for-the-opening-show-of-lakme-fashion-week-in-partnership-with-the-fashion-design-council-of-india/). Day one also ran at the Embassy of France, where the R|Elan Circular Design Challenge finale gave Margn by Saurabh Maurya the top prize and USD 16,000, per [FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.uk/news/fashion/r-elan-circular-design-challenge-2026-margn-takes-top-prize/2026101590856). The question is who paid and what they got for it.

## Maruti Suzuki NEXA presents Manish Malhotra

The show of the week was a sponsor's. Manish Malhotra came back after almost three and a half years on 17 October with his first menswear Luxe Prêt line, plus fine jewellery, per [t2](https://t2online.in/goodlife/fashion-beauty/manish-malhotra-s-menswear-luxe-pret-launches-with-sara-ali-khan-and-ayushmann-khurrana-as-showstoppers--at-lfw-x-fdci/2008722). Musician Donn Bhat ran the set, and a car made a grand entry ahead of the showstoppers, Sara Ali Khan and Ayushmann Khurrana, promoting Udta Teer, out 23 October, per [Republic World](https://www.republicworld.com/entertainment/ayushmann-khurrana-sara-ali-khan-turn-showstoppers-as-manish-malhotra-returns-to-lakme-fashion-week-2026-2026-10-18-139172). The show was presented by Maruti Suzuki NEXA, and the release says the collection drew inspiration from the New Age Baleno, per [Indian Retailer](https://indianretailer.com/brandlicense/archives/news/nexa-partners-manish-malhotra-luxury-pret-2026-lakme-fashion-week.n6130). The sponsor was in the clothes and on the ramp.

## American Express takes the front row

American Express came on as Experience Partner, per the release carried by [India CSR](https://indiacsr.in/?p=202051) and [Eastern Herald](https://easternherald.com/2026/10/17/american-express-comes-on-board-as-experience-partner-for-lakme-fashion-week-in-partnership-with-fashion-design-council-of-india-fdci/). Centurion and Platinum card members got front-row seats at select shows, a VIP lounge and hospitality, subject to terms. Everyone else got a brand experience zone inside Taj Palace. Jaspreet Chandok, Group Vice President at Reliance Brands, said the aim was to "build an experience that extends beyond the runway". The front row was once an editor's seat. It is now a card tier.

## House of Lakmé closes with an ice-cream cart

Amit Aggarwal's AM:IT closed the week with On a Day Like Today, per [Open](https://openthemagazine.com/art-culture/amit-aggarwal-brings-balloons-ice-cream-carts-and-a-burst-of-colour-to-the-lakmé-fashion-week-grand-finale): balloons, candyfloss, an ice-cream cart, and an auto rickshaw on the ramp, per [Hollywood Reporter India](https://www.hollywoodreporterindia.com/lifestyle/fashion/aneet-padda-closes-lakmē-fashion-week-x-fdci-grand-finale-with-amit). Aneet Padda, Lakmé's brand ambassador, closed the show. It was built around Lakmé 9to5's 5PM Glam Reset concept. A product launch, choreographed better than most.

## Ethnix by Raymond books the captain

Suryakumar Yadav made his runway debut on 18 October as showstopper for Ethnix by Raymond, per [ANI via newkerala](https://www.newkerala.com/news/a/kept-her-small-toys-my-wardrobe-suryakumar-yadav-522.htm), in an ivory Indo-Western with chintz-inspired embroidery. He turned to the crowd, recreated the Supla shot and mimed a guitar, per [Khel Now](https://khelnow.com/cricket/suryakumar-yadav-recreates-supla-shot-202610). A T20 World Cup winning captain in a menswear show is a media plan. A good one.

## The read

Delhi's edition ran like a media plan with a runway attached. NEXA got the car entry, Amex got the front row, Lakmé 9to5 got the finale, Raymond got the captain. None of it is a complaint: the sponsors paid and the sponsors showed, on the ramp rather than on a backdrop. The test is whether the clothes still earn the room. On the week's reports, Malhotra's menswear and Aggarwal's finale did. Mask360 was not invited. We read the receipts from outside.
