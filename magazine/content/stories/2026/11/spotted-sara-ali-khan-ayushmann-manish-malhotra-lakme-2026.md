## The look

Saturday 17 October, Taj Palace, New Delhi. Manish Malhotra shows Luxury Prêt 2026 at Lakmé Fashion Week x FDCI, presented by NEXA, his first show there in nearly three and a half years, per [ANI via Republic World](https://www.republicworld.com/entertainment/ayushmann-khurrana-sara-ali-khan-turn-showstoppers-as-manish-malhotra-returns-to-lakme-fashion-week-2026-2026-10-18-139172).

The showstoppers: Sara Ali Khan in a black strapless corset bodice with metallic floral embellishment, a flowing skirt and a sheer drape. Ayushmann Khurrana in a tailored black jacket with silver floral embroidery at the shoulders, lapels and sleeves. The two co-star in Udta Teer, slated for 23 October, so the walk did double duty.

The collection: cropped jackets, fluid skirts, relaxed tailoring, gold and burnished silver. It introduced Menswear Luxe Prêt and put the house's fine jewellery on the runway, per [UrbanAsian](https://urbanasian.com/lifestyle/fashion/2026/10/manish-malhotra-luxury-pret-2026-lakme-fashion-week/). A car entered ahead of the showstoppers, live percussion over a Donn Bhat set, per [t2online](https://t2online.in/goodlife/fashion-beauty/manish-malhotra-s-menswear-luxe-pret-launches-with-sara-ali-khan-and-ayushmann-khurrana-as-showstoppers--at-lfw-x-fdci/2008722).
