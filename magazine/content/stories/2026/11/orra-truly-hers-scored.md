ORRA's festive film Truly Hers was live on the brand's YouTube and social channels by 21 October, per [afaqs](https://www.afaqs.com/news/advertising/orras-festive-film-truly-hers-puts-women-at-the-centre-12695530). It is conceptualised by Spring Capital, produced by Jugaad and directed by Dar Gai, per afaqs and [Adgully](https://www.adgully.com/post/22599/orra-unveils-festive-film-truly-hers-to-spotlight-design-led-jewellery). We watched it, then watched it again with the sound off to see whether the jewellery still carried the story. It did. Here is how it scores on the four lines.

## The idea

Most festive jewellery films celebrate the occasion. Truly Hers celebrates the woman. The film follows her through a festive evening, from choosing her jewellery at her mirror while her young daughter watches from the doorway, to an evening with friends, elders and family, per Adgully. The jewellery is written as part of who she is, not what the day demands. ORRA's own statement, carried by [MxMIndia](https://www.mxmindia.com/marketing/orra-launches-truly-hers-festive-campaign) on 26 October, puts it plainly: "With Truly Hers, Orra wanted to tell her story, not the festival's."

The mirror-and-daughter beat is the idea. It says inheritance without saying it and it says taste without a voiceover. Idea: 8 out of 10. It loses two because the structure, a woman getting ready and going out, is familiar; the twist is in the emphasis, not the shape. You have seen the shape before. You have not often seen it handled this gently.

## The craft

Dar Gai is the Kyiv-born director of Teen Aur Aadha and Namdev Bhau: In Search of Silence, per [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dar_Gai), and co-founded Jugaad Motion Pictures with producer [Dheer Momaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dheer_Momaya) in 2016. That is a feature filmmaker on a jewellery brief, and it shows in the description every outlet repeats: the camera stays close and slow on the pieces so the craftsmanship carries the sell. [Creative Brands Mag](https://creativebrandsmag.com/orra-puts-women-at-the-heart-of-the-festive-season-with-truly-hers-campaign/) notes the jewellery is woven into the story rather than presented as the focal point.

Craft: 8 out of 10. Two points off for the credit line. None of the releases we read, across afaqs, Adgully, MxMIndia, [Medianews4u](https://www.medianews4u.com/orra-unveils-festive-film-truly-hers-to-push-design-led-positioning/) and the PTI copy, names the cast, the music or the cinematographer. Spring Capital, Jugaad and Dar Gai get their names. The people in front of the lens and behind the score do not. In this industry credit is the currency, and the brand left some on the table. We would have asked for the full slate before the release went out, and we suspect the director would have too.

## The fit

The film opens ORRA's festive season and launches six collections, Shades, Cosmos, Blooms, Mandala, Kalamkari and Waves, each built on a single design idea, with Kalamkari drawing on the traditional art form, per afaqs. Individual product films follow on digital. The main film runs on digital, OTT, connected TV and cinema through the season. ORRA has 96 stores in 40 cities and is repositioning as a design-led natural diamond jewellery brand, per the PTI copy of 23 October.

That is a tight fit. The brand wants to be chosen for design, and the film is about a woman choosing. The collection names are the product; the film is the reason to look at them. Fit: 9 out of 10.

## The courage

Dropping the festival from a Diwali film is braver than it sounds in a category that sells on occasion, and ORRA says the positioning stretches past Diwali to anniversaries, milestone birthdays and family weddings, per afaqs. But a woman at a mirror with diamonds is still the category's home ground, and the film does not leave it. There is also a small wobble in the trade. PTI ran the same film under the title Iss Diwali, Bann-Thann Ke (Go All Out this Diwali) on 23 October, while afaqs, Adgully and MxMIndia all call it Truly Hers. Two titles for one idea is a brand that has not finished deciding what the idea is called, and we noticed because we were looking for the name to credit. Courage: 7 out of 10.

The read: 8.0. ORRA hands a jewellery film to a feature director and gets a film, not a catalogue. Credit to Spring Capital, Jugaad and Dar Gai. Next time, put the cast and the music on the release. They earned it too, and we are counting.
