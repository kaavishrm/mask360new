## Geography

Primary: Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Goa. Secondary: Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Kolkata. A story outside these runs only if an Indian or Gulf brand is in it.

## Daily source list (the scout reads these every run)

Trade: afaqs, Campaign India, exchange4media, Social Samosa, Adgully, Best Media Info, Marketing Mind, Mad Over Marketing, ET BrandEquity, Storyboard18, Medianama (policy), ASCI complaint outcomes.
Business: Economic Times, Mint, Business Standard, Moneycontrol, Inc42, YourStory, VCCircle (deals), Entrackr.
Hospitality: Hospitality Biz India, Condé Nast Traveller India, Mint Lounge, LBB, Zomato and Swiggy blog and app announcements, Bangalore Mirror and Mid-day food pages, Gulf News and Time Out Dubai for the Gulf.
Fashion and beauty: Vogue India, GQ India, Grazia, The Established, The Nod, Harper's Bazaar India, Elle India, BoF for global brands entering India, Nykaa and Myntra announcements.
Culture: Hindustan Times entertainment, Film Companion, Rolling Stone India, Homegrown, ESPNcricinfo for sponsorship, Instagram and X for the moment itself.
Public filings: MCA filings, BSE and NSE announcements for listed brands, trademark registry for launches, ASCI orders, Delhi and Bombay High Court cause lists for brand disputes.
People: LinkedIn (public posts), agency press releases, Campaign India moves column.

Every source is read for the thing it did not say: the sponsor missing from the list, the CMO missing from the launch photo, the price that moved.

## Section rules

- **Campaigns** runs a Scorecard at least twice a week. Score the work that matters, not the work that is easy.
- **Events** always says who paid. If the event was invite-only and we were invited, the caption says so.
- **Hospitality** always prints the price of the signature thing. The Edit in this section always has "the catch".
- **Fashion** names the designer, the photographer and the stylist on SWEET stories. Credit is the currency of this industry.
- **People** never speculates on why someone left beyond what is on the record. It may note what the move says about the market.
- **Culture** keeps the celebrity secondary to the brand decision. We are not a celebrity title.

## Watchlist

The home page band "On the watchlist" (the reference's author band) lists the brands and people most covered in the last 30 days, generated by the build from story tags. The yellow band, italic serif names, monogram circles. Being on it is the industry's new scoreboard.
