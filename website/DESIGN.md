# Mask360 site: design note

## The idea
The quiet arrogance of a house that shows you the palace and does not explain it.

## References and the exact borrow
- Locomotive and Immersive Garden: scroll narrative pacing, atmosphere over spectacle, one peak motion (the hero loop), everything else still.
- Bureau Borsche: the editorial grid, one grotesk set huge and tight against small tracked captions, hairline rules.
- Ssense: monochrome, the work on plain grounds, an index as the interface for the work list, product-grade restraint.
- Aman: emptiness photographed as abundance. The Anantara pavilion at dusk is the first image for that reason.
- The module reference (the 2026 Edition board): the card system, curved corners, dark and light alternation, photo cells that interrupt the grid, numbers only on a true sequence.

## What we are not doing
Service grids with icons. "We are a 360 agency" as the hero. A logo wall above the fold. Team photos. A button on every scroll. Gradients, glow, purple, glass. Fade-up on every section, hover lift on every card. Carousels. Numbers on content that is not a sequence. Exclamation marks, "elevate", "seamless", "journey", "unlock", em dashes, emojis.

## Tokens
- Type: Space Grotesk only, self hosted, 300 to 700. Display 700 at minus 3 percent tracking, leading 0.95. h2 600. Body 300 with 500 for bold. Micro labels 400 at plus 12 percent tracking, uppercase, 11px. Italic flame accent inside headlines and ledes.
- Colour: white #FFFFFF ground, paper #F3F2EE alternate, ink #0A0A0A, night #0A0A0A ground with #161616 cards, flame #F53901. Hairlines at 12 percent ink or 14 percent white.
- Radius: 20px cards, 14px images, pills for the two buttons on the site.
- Space: gutters 6vw desktop and 16px phone, sections 14vh apart, margins twice what feels safe.
- Motion: the hero loop, a slow scale on a case hero, underlines. Nothing else moves. Reduced motion respected.
- Image: real photography and real frames from the work, matte, no filters, no stock, no AI renders presented as photographs.
- Copy: sentence case, few words, no adjectives selling, the client as hero, one founder note in lowercase as the human gesture.
